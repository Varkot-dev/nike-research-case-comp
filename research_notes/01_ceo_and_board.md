## 1. Elliott Hill (President and CEO since Oct 14, 2024)

### Timeline
- 1988: Joins Nike as an apparel sales intern/rep (TCU and Ohio University grad). https://yourstory.com/2025/02/elliott-hill-nike-intern-journey-ceo
- 1998: Director, Team Sports Division (one aggregator source; verify).
- 2000: Moves to Europe as VP Sales and Retail, EMEA (aggregator source; mid-career dates vary across sources).
- 2016: President, Geographies and Integrated Marketplace (aggregator; verify). 
- 2018 to 2020: President, Consumer and Marketplace, overseeing all commercial and marketing operations for Nike and Jordan Brand. https://www.cnbc.com/2024/09/19/nike-ceo-john-donahoe-is-out-replaced-by-elliott-hill.html
- June 2020: Retires, a few months after Donahoe became CEO. Hill says it was not because he was passed over; he cited needing a physical break. https://fortune.com/article/nike-ceo-veteran-elliott-hill-turnaround-growth/
- 2020 to 2024: Founded Open Road Resources, advised a merchant bank, sat on several boards (aggregator: https://yourstory.com/2025/02/elliott-hill-nike-intern-journey-ceo).
- Summer 2024: Mark Parker phones Hill; conversations "went from light to a lot heavier very quickly." Board had weighed other internal and external candidates first, partly unsure Hill was interested. https://fortune.com/article/nike-ceo-veteran-elliott-hill-turnaround-growth/
- Sept 19, 2024: Nike announces Hill as CEO effective Oct 14. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018724000053/exhibit991-pressreleasesep.htm and https://www.sportico.com/business/commerce/2024/nike-ceo-john-donahoe-out-elliott-hill-1234798018/

### Approach and signals
- "Win Now" plan: sport-first, repair wholesale relationships, flatten layers, use veterans and retirees ("putting the band back together", https://sgbonline.com/exec-nike-putting-the-band-back-together-as-elliott-hill-named-ceo-effective-oct-14/). Contrast with Donahoe: outsider, digital/DTC-first.
- Org shift: regional leaders elevated to the top team; commercial and sales moved under the CFO; COO created. Signals a push for accountability by market and operational tightening.
- Pattern: heavy churn of senior team after year 1 (O'Neill, Williams, Dogan, Grebert, Dong, Heaf, Wilkins) even among insiders.

### Confidence
High: 1988 start, 2020 retirement, Oct 14 2024 start, Parker call, Dec 2025 changes. Medium: May 2025 and early 2026 moves (secondary summaries). Low/unverified: Hill's 1998/2000/2016 role titles and dates, 2026 exec exits' dates, stock/earnings figures ("market value more than halved" is a Bloomberg-syndicated claim).

## 2. John Donahoe (CEO Jan 13, 2020 to Oct 13, 2024)

### Timeline
- ~1984 to 2005: Bain and Company, 20+ years; CEO of Bain 1999 to 2005. https://conferences.law.stanford.edu/directorscollege2026/speakers/john-donahoe-2/
- 2005: eBay (president, eBay Marketplaces); CEO 2008 to 2015. Chaired PayPal board 2015 to 2024.
- 2014: Joins Nike board.
- 2017 to 2020: CEO ServiceNow.
- Oct 2019: Named Nike CEO via succession process led by board member Tim Cook; starts Jan 13, 2020. https://retailwire.com/discussion/will-nikes-new-ceo-accelerate-its-consumer-direct-digital-transformation/
- June 2020: Consumer Direct Acceleration (CDA): "marketplace of the future," category realignment, digital investment, job cuts, July 2020 leadership shuffle. Same link as above plus https://ca.fashionnetwork.com/news/Nike-makes-mass-senior-leadership-changes-announces-job-cuts,1233295.html
- May 2023: Senior team reshuffle (Craig Williams to lead geographies and marketplace after Jordan Brand). https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/nike-reshuffles-senior-leadership-team-to-drive-greater-integration/2023052569732
- Dec 2023: ~$2B three-year cost-cutting plan.
- Early 2024: Publicly acknowledged Nike had gone too far from wholesale partners.
- Sept 19, 2024: Retirement announced after a record-bad trading day on weak guidance; Nike said no disagreement. Phil Knight had earlier voiced "unwavering confidence." Advisor through Jan 2025. https://www.cnbc.com/2024/09/19/nike-ceo-john-donahoe-is-out-replaced-by-elliott-hill.html and https://www.opb.org/article/2024/09/19/nike-ceo-business-john-donahoe-elliott-hill/
- July 31, 2025: Named Stanford athletics director. https://conferences.law.stanford.edu/directorscollege2026/speakers/john-donahoe-2/ (cited via search summary)

### Hires
Search results did not verify specific hires. Matt Friend (CFO) and Heidi O'Neill, Craig Williams appear as Donahoe-era leaders in the sources above, but hire dates were NOT confirmed here. [UNVERIFIED] Class action alleged Donahoe and Friend misled investors on DTC success (retailwire link).

### Signals
Tech-platform playbook (DTC, data, membership) cut Nike's wholesale base and weakened product pipeline; the board reversed it by returning to a product/sport/wholesale veteran.

### Confidence
High: career arc, dates of tenure, CDA, exit framing. Unverified: whether board forced exit (nobody confirmed; official line is mutual decision), his Nike hires, the exact start year at Bain.

## 3. Mark Parker (CEO 2006 to Jan 2020; Executive Chairman)

- 1979: Joins Nike as footwear designer/product tester; later VP consumer product marketing, VP global footwear, co-president Nike Brand. https://about.nike.com/en/company/people/mark-parker
- 2006: Becomes third CEO. 
- 2015/2016: Succeeds Phil Knight as chairman (sources say announced June 2015, effective 2016; verify). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Parker
- Oct 2019: Announces move to Executive Chairman effective Jan 13, 2020.
- Jan 2023: Disney board chair per Wikipedia-derived summary; reportedly left Disney board Jan 2025 [UNVERIFIED].
- Sept 2024: Quoted as Executive Chairman announcing Hill; personally phoned Hill in summer 2024.
- Sept 16, 2026: Still Executive Chairman, quoted on Arnault appointment. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000170/a091626pressrelease.htm
- Signal: Parker bookends the product-led era; he picked Donahoe (2019, with Cook's process) and then Hill (2024). The chair remains the key decision-maker; accountability for both choices sits with him. That is my inference, not a sourced claim.
