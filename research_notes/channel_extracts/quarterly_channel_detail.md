## (C) Caveats

1. **Coverage gaps.** Q4 FY19, Q1 FY20 and Q2 FY20 releases give no quarterly NIKE Direct or wholesale figures, only directional "growth across NIKE Direct and wholesale". Q3 FY20 gives only NIKE Direct currency-neutral growth (+13%) and digital (+36%). Q4 FY20 gives digital and wholesale shipment volume but no channel revenue. Q4 FY19 to Q4 FY23 Q4 release channel tables (p. 5 (seq)) are full-year only. Q4 FY21 text states Q4 NIKE Direct (+73%) but only directional wholesale (triple-digit). Q4 FY22 onward texts state Q4 NIKE Direct and wholesale directly, and those figures are used here. For Q4 FY19 and Q4 FY20 a Q4 split would have to be derived as FY minus nine months, which this file does not do.
2. **"Digital" label changes.** Through Q4 FY20 the releases say "digital sales" or "digital commerce sales". From Q1 FY21 they say "NIKE Brand digital sales" and from Q1 FY22 "NIKE Brand Digital". Converse digital is separate and was never quantified.
3. **Digital growth basis.** From Q4 FY23 the releases give a single digital growth number with no basis. The 10-Qs place the same number inside the currency-neutral NIKE Direct sentence (e.g. Q1 FY25 10-Q seq 24). Treat `digital_growth` rows from Q4 FY23 onward as currency-neutral by context. Before that, releases state both reported and currency-neutral.
4. **Stores vs digital reclassification.** The Q1, Q2 and Q3 FY24 10-Qs say "certain reclassifications made between NIKE-owned retail stores and NIKE Brand Digital in the prior period" (not material). Q1 FY27 shows Q1 FY26 digital as $2.1B, while the Q1 FY26 10-Q reported $2.0B (seq 24). This could be rounding or an unflagged reclass; it is not explained.
5. **Wholesale equivalent removed.** Starting Q1 FY25 the non-GAAP "wholesale equivalent revenues" measure was dropped after the ERP rollout (Q1 FY25 10-Q seq 21). The FY24 gross margin bridges (off-price, NIKE Direct margin bps) use wholesale-equivalent framing. From Q1 FY25 the bridges use "NIKE Brand ASP" with channel mix inside it, so the bps are not like-for-like across that break.
6. **Store metric change.** Through Q3 FY26 the 10-Qs report "comparable store sales". From Q1 FY26 they also report "NIKE store sales" growth. The Q1 FY27 10-Q reports only "NIKE store sales" ($2.3B, -5%) and no comparable store sales figure was found. ER "NIKE-owned stores" growth equals 10-Q "store sales" growth, not comps.
7. **Wholesale label.** ERs through Q4 FY26 say "Wholesale revenues"; Q1 FY27 says "NIKE Brand wholesale revenues". Both exclude Converse. Converse channel growth (DTC vs wholesale) comes from 10-Q segment narrative only, all currency-neutral.
8. **Gross margin comparability.** The Q4 FY26 +890 bps includes about 900 bps of expected IEEPA tariff recovery. FY26 Q1 to Q3 margins carry new North America tariffs (Q2 FY26 -360 bps). Q3 FY24 and Q3 FY25 bridges include restructuring effects (50 bps). Channel-mix bps are seldom isolated; usually they are bundled with discounts inside ASP.
9. **Rounding.** Release $ figures are in billions to one decimal. Growth rates are management-rounded and can differ from figures computed off the 10-Q note tables (not re-extracted here per instructions).
10. **COVID base effects.** FY21 growth (e.g. Q4 FY21 NIKE Direct +73%, wholesale triple-digit) laps store closures. Q4 FY21 also compares against Q4 FY19.
11. **Untrusted text.** All content is quoted from filings as data. No instructions inside the filings were followed.
