# Nike's standing as of October 13, 2026: two years into Elliott Hill's tenure

Conventions used in these notes:
- Filing numbers reuse the repo's verified data (`research_notes/04_financial_data_pack.md`, `data/nike_filing_facts.csv`, `REPORT.md`) and its citation style, for example (Q1 FY27 10-Q p. 25). Filing links are given in each row or bullet.
- "Opened" means I opened the page in this session. "Repo note" means the item comes from another repo research file whose author opened the page; I did not re-open it. "Snippet only" means I saw only a search-result summary.
- The Q1 FY27 earnings call transcript is Nike's official PDF, which I opened and read in full: [Nike Q1 FY27 official transcript, Oct 1, 2026 (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf). It is cited below as "Q1 FY27 call".

To line up with the peers: Adidas quarter 1 = Q1 2023 (Gulden started Jan 2023); Under Armour quarter 1 = the quarter that began April 2024 (Plank returned April 2024).

## 1. Quarter-by-quarter scorecard since Hill (Q2 FY2025 to Q1 FY2027)

### Takeaway
After eight quarters under Hill, Nike's revenue is still shrinking (Q1 FY27 down 4% reported, 5% currency-neutral) and guided to fall high-single digits for FY27; the only sustained positives are North America (growth in every quarter since Q1 FY26) and the wholesale rebuild, which itself turned negative again in Q1 FY27. Gross margin has stayed at roughly 40% to 43% excluding the one-off tariff refund, versus 44% to 45% before Hill, and Greater China has declined for nine straight quarters.

### Inferences
- The shape of the Hill era: four quarters of steep decline as Nike cleared inventory and cut classic franchises (quarters 1 to 3, revenue down 8% to 12%), then four quarters of roughly flat reported revenue driven by North America wholesale (quarters 4 to 7), then a relapse in quarter 8 with guidance for further decline. There is no quarter yet in which currency-neutral revenue grew meaningfully.
- Benchmarking note for the report writer: by quarter 8, Gulden's Adidas was already growing again on a currency-neutral basis (this is the comparison other researchers cover). Nike at quarter 8 looks more like a company still in the "clean-up" phase, which is closer to Under Armour's path than Adidas's.
- The ex-refund gross margin (about 40% in Q4 FY26, 42.8% in Q1 FY27) is still 200 to 450 bps below pre-Hill levels; the Q1 FY27 improvement came from logistics costs and FX rather than full-price selling (ASP was a drag).

### Gaps
- Nike does not report quarterly currency-neutral wholesale growth consistently in the filing CSV for every quarter (Q2 FY26, Q4 FY26, Q1 FY27 c-n wholesale missing); reported figures are used.
- Q4 quarterly NIKE Direct and wholesale dollar values exist only rounded to billions.

## 2. Q1 FY2027 detail and management commentary (reported Oct 1, 2026)

### Takeaway
Management split Nike into "two different realities": a growing Performance business (about $16B in FY26, up high-single digits in Q1, with running, football, tennis and golf up double digits) and a shrinking Sportswear (just under half of revenue, down low double digits), Jordan (13% of revenue, down mid-teens) and Greater China (down 26% c-n). New CFO Dave Denton guided FY27 revenue down high-single digits, EBIT down more than revenue, adjusted EPS $1.15 to $1.35, a roughly 400 bps Q2 revenue headwind, and said the pain will "bleed" into FY28.

### Cited Findings
**What grew** (all from [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf), Hill unless noted)
- NIKE Performance was "$16 billion" in FY26 (grew mid-single digits) and grew "by another high single digits" in Q1; Denton: excluding Greater China it would have been "up low double digits."
- "Running is up double digits again with consistent share gains." "Led by the strength of the Vomero franchise, we've nearly tripled our share of the Max cushioning category over the past year." In China running is in its "sixth quarter of growth."
- New running launches: Pegasus Plus 2 (August), Alphafly 4 (September), Swooshfly entry-level marathon shoe and NIKE Apex super shoe ("40% more energy than the Alphafly 3") on Sept 30.
- Football: "strong double-digit growth in all 4 geos"; "we doubled our World Cup team kits sales compared to the 2022 tournament"; club football kits "up high teens."
- Basketball "up double digits in North America"; women's signature business up "nearly 500% from FY '22 to FY '26"; the Caitlin 1 is "the largest women's signature shoe launch in NIKE's history," in "5,000 doors, twice the average"; SHAI shoe launched the same day.
- Training grew globally (Nike Mind "one of our top-selling franchises"; new Hybrid footwear system); tennis and golf grew double digits.
- Within Sportswear: Air Force 1 "driving a stable full-price business"; P-6000 and V5 Runner grew "strong double digits"; Studio Fleece was "the best-performing apparel collection of the quarter in all of NIKE, Inc."
- North America +2% "driven by performance led by Running, Global Football and Basketball," with "good sell-through" at DICK'S, Academy, SCHEELS, JD and Foot Locker.

**What shrank**
- Sportswear, "just under half of this quarter's revenue, was down low double digits." "We reduced revenue from the Dunk by nearly 50% in the quarter. That resulted in roughly $200 million headwind." "Some aged, higher volume, sportswear footwear sold through below expectations." "There's a lack of energy in the lifestyle space right now, which is impacting traffic." [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf)
- Jordan: "represented 13% of our global business with revenue falling by mid-teens." "We've been oversupplying our iconic retro product." Nike will "reduce the volume and frequency of specific Jordan Retro launches"; "North America will feel the biggest impact." [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf)
- Denton: Sportswear and Jordan "represented a high single-digit drag on consolidated NIKE." [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf)
- Converse revenue -28% to $263M; Converse DTC -33%, wholesale -26% (Q1 FY27 10-Q p. 25, 30). [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000193/nke-20260831.htm)
- Greater China -22% reported, -26% c-n, to $1,180M; China wholesale -28%, China NIKE Direct -13%, China digital -28% c-n (Q1 FY27 10-Q p. 25, 28). Hill: Nike is "eliminating distribution through channels that are not aligned with our marketplace strategy"; "the majority of our partners' physical doors in Greater China have not been refreshed in the past 7 years"; first made-for-China collection launches in October, brick-and-mortar only. Denton: guidance "assumes that China actually gets worse from a revenue perspective for the balance of this year." [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000193/nke-20260831.htm); [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf)
- EMEA -5%, with EMEA digital -26% c-n (Q1 FY27 10-Q p. 25, 27). [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000193/nke-20260831.htm)

**Order book and wholesale**
- Hill: below-expectation sell-through of aged Sportswear "has impacted our future order books as we proactively work with our wholesale partners to work through excess inventory." [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf)
- Wholesale revenue fell 1% in Q1 FY27 to $6,804M after five quarters of growth (Q1 FY27 10-Q p. 22). [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000193/nke-20260831.htm)
- No numeric futures/order-book figure was given (Nike stopped disclosing futures years ago; nothing on the call).

**Guidance (Denton)**
- FY27: "revenues to decline in the high single-digit range"; "EBIT to decline by a greater percentage than revenue"; "continued pressure on gross margin and fixed cost deleverage"; SG&A "down year-over-year"; adjusted EPS "$1.15 to $1.35," excluding about $0.15 of Pace costs. Nike will now give full-year guidance and update it, rather than quarterly guidance. [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf); (Q1 FY27 release p. 1) [release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000184/q1fy27exhibit991er.htm)
- Q2 FY27: lapping EMEA Cyber Week promotions and a higher North America sell-in comparison "will represent a headwind of approximately 400 basis points to revenues in the second quarter." No gross margin guidance. [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf). Note for repo maintainers: the data pack (section 7) dropped this 400 bps figure as unverified; it is verbatim in Nike's official transcript.
- Duration: the actions will "bleed in a little bit into fiscal '28" (Denton, answering JPMorgan's "pain before gain" question). [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf)

**Pace restructuring plan**
- About $2.5B cumulative savings through FY2031, about $1.0B pre-tax charges (mostly employee-related) plus about $0.3B severance already booked in FY26 (8-K filed Oct 1, 2026, Item 2.05, p. 2; Q1 FY27 release p. 1). [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000320187/000032018726000184/nke-20261001.htm)
- Denton: "the majority of the program savings to be realized in fiscal years '29 and '30," with a portion reinvested; some supply-chain savings are already visible in Q1 gross margin. Elements: flexible supply chain, a new Bengaluru campus, four geographies to three (Americas, APGC, EMEA) in FY28, and changes that "will reduce the overall number of roles." [Q1 FY27 call (opened)](https://s1.q4cdn.com/806093406/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NIKE-Inc-Q1FY27-OFFICIAL-Transcript_-FINAL.pdf)
- Nike says it does "not yet know the number of roles"; role decisions "will begin in calendar year 2027" (Nike release, Oct 1, 2026, per REPORT.md section 4). [REPORT.md source list; 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000320187/000032018726000184/nke-20261001.htm)

### Inferences
- The turnaround now rests on Performance growing fast enough to outrun a deliberate shrink of Sportswear, Jordan and China; at roughly $16B against a company of about $46B, Hill concedes it is "not yet large enough."
- Denton's guidance style (annual only, "reasonable and attainable," built after a "bottoms up" review) reads as a kitchen-sink reset ahead of Investor Day, similar to what new CFOs typically do.

### Gaps
- Nike gave no category dollar figures for running, basketball or Vomero/Pegasus in Q1 FY27, only directional rates.
- No numeric order-book or futures data is published.

## 3. Leading indicators of a turn

### Takeaway
Product and sport indicators are positive (running share gains, World Cup kit sales doubled, record women's basketball launch), but marketplace-health indicators are mixed to negative: order books hit by excess Sportswear inventory, discounts increased in Q1, inventory up 5% since May, a major retailer complaining about brand-site promotions, and the loss of Mbappe to On.
