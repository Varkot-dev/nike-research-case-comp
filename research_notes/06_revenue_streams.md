# Nike revenue streams: is there anything beyond DTC and wholesale?

**Prepared:** October 13, 2026. **Sources:** FY2026 Form 10-K and Q1 FY2027 Form 10-Q, via `data/nike_filing_facts.csv` and `data/nike_channel_split.csv` (page-level citations and verbatim quotes in both files). All shares are my arithmetic on filed figures. Chart: `charts/15_revenue_mix_fy26.png`.

**Short answer:** no. In FY2026, 99.7% of NIKE, Inc. revenue ($46,398M, FY26 10-K p. 55) was product sold either to retailers (wholesale) or directly to consumers (NIKE Direct). Licensing and other income was $143M, or 0.3% (FY26 10-K p. 83). The useful "other" splits are by product, by brand (Jordan) and by region, not by income type.

## 1. By channel (FY2026)

| Channel | $M | Share of revenue | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales to wholesale customers (incl. Converse) | 28,058 | 60.5% | FY26 10-K p. 83 |
| Nike-owned stores | about 9,100 | 19.6% | FY26 10-K p. 33 ($9.1B) |
| NIKE Brand Digital | about 8,600 | 18.5% | FY26 10-K p. 33 ($8.6B) |
| Converse direct to consumer | 477 | 1.0% | FY26 10-K p. 41 |
| Licensing and other | 143 | 0.3% | FY26 10-K p. 83 |
| **Total** | **46,398** | **100%** | FY26 10-K p. 55 |

- Stores plus Digital equal NIKE Brand Direct of $17,720M (FY26 10-K p. 32); the store and digital figures are disclosed only rounded to $0.1B.
- "Other" is Global Brand Divisions ($49M), Converse licensing and other ($92M, FY26 10-K p. 41) and Corporate ($2M, FY26 10-K p. 32). Whatever its exact makeup, Nike has no royalty or licensing income of any size.
- Q1 FY2027 (total $11,213M): wholesale 62.0%, NIKE Direct 37.8%, other 0.2% (Q1 FY27 10-Q p. 14).
- Channel history (Direct share rising from 28% to a 43.7% peak, then falling) is in `05_channel_split.md`.

## 3. By brand: Jordan

- Jordan Brand revenue was **$7,034M in FY2026, down 3%**, about 15% of NIKE, Inc. revenue (FY26 10-K p. 32). It is included in the NIKE Brand footwear and apparel lines above, not additional to them.
- Jordan is the only sub-brand Nike breaks out, which makes it the largest disclosed stream outside the channel and product splits.
- **Caution:** Jordan figures have been restated. FY2024 Jordan was $6,988M in the FY24 10-K (p. 34) but $8,701M as recast in the Q4 FY25 release. Use one filing's figures for any trend. A Jordan deep dive is in progress.

## 5. What this means for the thesis (my inference)
1. Revenue is concentrated: two channels, one dominant product (footwear, 64%), one dominant region (North America, 44%), and rising retailer concentration (top three U.S. customers 29% of U.S. sales in FY2026, see `05_channel_split.md` Section 6).
2. There is no licensing, royalty or services income to cushion a product downturn. Any recovery case has to come from footwear sell-through, the wholesale and Direct mix, and Jordan.
3. Jordan, at about 15% of revenue, is the one brand-level lever large enough to move the consolidated numbers on its own.
