# Nike new products and launches, Jan 2024 to Oct 13, 2026

Prepared Oct 13, 2026 under the verify-non-sec-claims rules. Every row in the main table was checked against a page that was actually opened, and a verbatim quote was captured. Items seen only in search snippets are kept separately at the end.

## Summary

From early 2025 Nike's launch calendar was rebuilt around performance sport, led by running. Management's own figures: the Vomero 18 became a "$100 million dollar plus franchise" within about 90 days of its Feb 2025 launch. Nike Running grew over 20% in Q1, Q2 and Q3 FY26 and then posted "five consecutive quarters of double-digit growth", which added "roughly a Billion dollars". The Vomero franchise "nearly tripled" Nike's share of max cushioning. Newer platforms include Nike Mind (Jan 2026, "sold out in all geographies"), Aero-FIT football kits for the 2026 World Cup, ACG, Nike Hybrid (hybrid racing) and the women's signature shoes A'One (May 2025) and Caitlin 1 (Oct 2026, 5,000 doors, "the largest women's signature shoe launch in Nike's history").

On channel, almost every Nike newsroom release names both nike.com and "select retailers" or "select retail partners". Few launches were Direct-only. There are four clear exceptions:

- **Wholesale-first:** the Structure Plus launched "exclusively at running specialty stores worldwide" four weeks before nike.com, and the Vomero Premium reached "select retail partners" two weeks before its global release.
- **Partner-specific:** management cited a 24.7 training collection with Dick's and the Air Max 95 with JD as wholesale partnership examples. JD was the only wholesale partner to host the Air Max Dn8 launch experience.
- **New wholesale channel:** Nike returned to Amazon in the U.S. with a brand store in fall 2025.
- **China first:** LeBron XXIII, Caitlin 1 and Nike Hybrid RN each released in China one to two weeks before the global launch. The Swooshfly is planned the same way.

Management says it is moving away from a "Nike direct-first offense" toward an "integrated, elevated marketplace". It segments wholesale partners by sport, and it is cutting China's online distribution down to Nike flagships on Tmall, JD.com and Douyin from January 2027. Nike Sportswear, Jordan Brand, Converse and Greater China remain weak. In Q1 FY27 Nike cut Dunk revenue by nearly 50% and said it would reduce the volume and frequency of Jordan retro launches.

## What management says about the Sport Offense and allocating product between Direct and wholesale

1. **What it is.** In Jun 2025 Hill said Nike would align "our teams to lead with sport through what we are calling the sport offense". The earnings release lists four aims: "driving distinction within key sports, building a complete product portfolio, creating stories to inspire and connect with consumers, and elevating and growing the entire marketplace". The Men's, Women's and Kids structure was replaced by cross-functional sport teams spanning Nike, Jordan and Converse. About 8,000 employees were realigned in Q1 FY26. Running moved first, then Global Football. EMEA activated the structure on Dec 1, 2025. Sources: Q4 FY25 release (SEC Ex. 99.1), T-Q4FY25, T-Q1FY26, T-Q2FY26.
2. **Product construct.** Running was rebuilt into three silos: Pegasus, Vomero and Structure. Nike says this "allows us to introduce at least one new major running footwear style each season". Football uses the same model with three boot silos at multiple price points (Phantom, Tiempo, Mercurial). Sportswear is being split into "smaller areas of focus" (T-Q1FY26, T-Q1FY27).
3. **Direct versus wholesale.**
   - Jun 2025: "We'll invest in Nike Direct, digital & physical, and thoughtfully segment wholesale partners to serve specific sport consumers across channels and up-and-down price points" (T-Q4FY25).
   - Mar 2026: "For years, we were running a Nike direct-first offense. Now, we are rebalancing our offense through an integrated, elevated marketplace" (T-Q3FY26).
   - Partner segmentation by channel type: Dick's and Academy in sporting goods; Foot Locker and JD in athletic specialty; plus running specialty, football specialty and city specialty partners (T-Q3FY26).
   - New distribution: Amazon (U.S.), Urban Outfitters, more than 200 new women's-led doors including Aritzia, and running specialty (Fleet Feet). In Q4 FY25, 30 run-specialty accounts were hosted for a preview of upcoming running innovations (T-Q4FY25).
   - Nike Direct's role is to be a "premium destination" tied to sport moments and launches, with fewer promotion days. Digital traffic was expected to be down double digits in FY26 as nike.com moved to a full-price model (T-Q4FY25).
4. **Results by channel (earnings releases, company figures).**
   - Wholesale grew in Q1 FY26 (+5% currency-neutral), Q2 FY26 (+8%), Q3 FY26 (+1%) and Q4 FY26 (+1%), then fell 1% in Q1 FY27.
   - NIKE Direct declined every quarter from Q4 FY25 (-14%) to Q1 FY27 (-9%).
   - In FY26, wholesale revenue grew 4%. More than 15,000 wholesale spaces and 150+ Nike stores were refreshed (T-Q4FY26).
5. **Pace (Oct 2026).** Pace is the operating-model program meant to "accelerate and scale the success of the Sport Offense". It plans three geographies: Americas, APGC and EMEA. It targets about $2.5 billion in cumulative savings through FY31, at about $1.0 billion in charges (Q1 FY27 release; T-Q1FY27).
