# Smoking-gun hunt: what twelve forensic hunts found (Oct 15, 2026)

Question: is there a single fact in free data that by itself decides long or short on Nike? Twelve hunts (G1 to G12) were run on angles the repo had not tried, each told to list every alternative explanation and to try at least five routes before calling anything blocked. The one candidate that looked decisive (G12) was handed to a separate skeptic (G12_check) and did not survive as a smoking gun. Every note is in this folder with its routes log; CSVs and scripts sit beside each note or under `data/hunt/`, `data/credit/`, `scripts/`.

## Verdict

**No smoking gun.** No single fact decides the call. What the hunt produced instead is four filing-based findings that make the SHORT more defensible, one finding that strengthens the LONG, and two contradictions resolved. The pitch should present a mosaic, with the required margin first.

## Alternatives that were ruled out along the way
- Channel stuffing financed by credit (G2): no DSO or reserve fingerprint in Q1 and Q2 FY26.
- Accounting fraud (G4, G12): no restatement, no SEC inquiry since 2014, sales-related reserves never a critical audit matter, reserve ratio still above its FY19 to FY24 norm.
- Credit stress (G5): ruled out.
- Hidden price collapse (G3): price is down only about 3% from the FY24 peak.
- Thin product pipeline (G7): ruled out; the opposite is true.
- Hidden tax-rate risk (G8): ruled out; Pillar Two already applies with no material impact.

## What would still settle the open questions
Sell-through by brand (Circana or GfK); NA receivables and the returns-reserve rollforward (ask at Investor Day, Nov 16 to 17, or read the Q2 FY27 10-Q); the Belgian judgment after Nov 30; a multi-year nike.com traffic series (paid Similarweb or Semrush); I/B/E/S segment consensus.
