# N5: China e-commerce festival rankings (618 and 11.11), 2018 to 2026

Written 2026-10-15. Tags: OPENED (we read the page or file itself, including chart images), SNIPPET (search summary only), DERIVED (our arithmetic or chart measurement). Data: `N5_festival_rank_panel.csv` (24 rows), `N5_threeplatform_share_vs_gc_digital.csv`, `N5_brand_share_q2_q4_2025.csv`, `N5_routes_log.csv`. Builds on `research_notes/Long or short cruxes/china_doors.md` (doors not redone) and `research_notes/Smoking gun hunt/00_HUNT_SYNTHESIS.md`. Nike China digital figures come from the repo call transcripts (`data/hunt/earnings_calls/transcripts/`) and 10-Qs (`data/nike_filing_facts.csv`).

## Verdict

**No smoking gun.** Three results matter, and the second one is a correction to the simple story.

1. **Nike lost the top Tmall slot in 2025 and has not regained it.** Nike was #1 on every full-festival Tmall sports list we could read from 2019 and 2021 618 through 2024 (11.11 2021 varies by method: 2nd on presale, 3rd on a spot-only list). It was #3 on the 11.11 2025 full-cycle list (FILA 1, adidas 2) and #3 on the 618 2026 first-phase and full-cycle lists (adidas 1, FILA 2). OPENED, Tmall lists via Tianxia Wangshang.
2. **Nike's brand sales on Tmall, JD and Douyin fell much more slowly than Nike's reported Greater China digital revenue.** In the last three calendar quarters of 2025, Nike-brand sales across the three platforms fell 9% to 15% YoY while Nike Greater China Digital fell 27% to 36% (currency-neutral). Over three years the first is up 43% and the second is down 52% (details below). The gap is explained in the data by two things: Nike's own Tmall flagship fell 33% to 53% while partner-run and JD stores did not, and Nike's platform sales were rising on partner-operated stores while Nike-operated digital shrank.
3. **The share loss is real but smaller than the revenue loss.** Nike's share of three-platform sports apparel and footwear sales went from 11.4% (Q4 2024) to 9.7% (Q4 2025), a 15% relative fall, with units -6% and price (ASP) -10% in Q4 2025 (DERIVED). adidas moved 8.9% to 8.5%, Anta 6.8% to 6.0%, Li-Ning 7.7% to 7.4%.

## 3. Test: did Nike's festival rank or share fall faster or slower than its Greater China digital revenue?

**Share and sales: slower, by a lot.**
- Last year (Q4 2024 to Q4 2025): share -15% relative (11.4% to 9.7%); platform sales -15.5%; Nike GC Digital -36% in Q2 FY26 (the matching quarter). Platform sales fell 20.5pp less than digital.
- Three years (4Q22 base to 4Q25, DERIVED): platform sales index 1.283 x 1.315 x 0.846 = 1.43 (up 43%). GC Digital index (Q2 FY23 base) 0.78 x 0.96 x 0.64 = 0.48 (down 52%). Ratio 2.98.
- Before 2025 the gap was -34pp to -51pp in five of six quarters (platform sales +17% to +31% while digital -4% to -34%); in 2025 it narrowed to -17pp to -21pp as platform sales turned negative.

**Rank: faster, but not measurable.** Nike fell two places on Tmall (#1 in 11.11 2024 to #3 in 11.11 2025) in the year digital fell 36%. A rank cannot be set against a percentage: Nike was #1 on the 618 2025 list (secondary source) in the same quarter its Tmall flagship was down 53%. We therefore use share and sales, not rank, as the test, and treat rank as a direction signal only.

**Why digital revenue fell faster than brand GMV (alternatives tested):**

| Explanation | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Nike's own flagship collapsed while the rest of the brand's sales held up | Tmall NIKE flagship -33.0% (Q4 2025) against Nike GC Digital -36%; JD Nike stores +52% to +240%; Douyin partner store -7% | **Survives, main driver.** Own-flagship decline matches digital revenue within 3pp in the matching quarter |
| Partner-operated stores carry GMV that Nike books as wholesale, not Direct | Douyin plus JD TOPSPORTS stores equal 16.7% of Nike three-platform sales in Q4 2025 (RMB 0.73bn, DERIVED); Nike's GC wholesale grew through FY24 (+12% Q3 FY24, +19% Q2 FY24) while digital fell | **Survives, second driver.** Explains why GMV rose while Nike digital fell in FY24 to FY25 |
| GMV is gross (price times quantity paid) and Nike Digital is net of returns and discounts | Cannot separate; vendor does not say whether refunds are netted | **Open.** Would explain a level gap, not a trend gap |
| Currency | Both series are RMB or currency-neutral | Ruled out |
| Platform mix shift Tmall to Douyin | Nike Tmall share of its three-platform sales 42.1% to 31.7% (Q2) and 46.5% to 40.0% (Q4) while Nike's share of the whole three-platform category fell too | **Partly real but not the explanation of share loss.** Nike lost share on the combined total, so mix shift alone cannot produce it |

Greater China total revenue for context (data pack): Q2 FY23 $1,788M, Q2 FY24 $1,863M, Q2 FY25 $1,711M, Q2 FY26 $1,423M (index 100, 104, 96, 80, reported USD). Nike's total China revenue is down 20% over the same stretch in which its three-platform brand GMV is up 43%, which says Nike's revenue capture of platform GMV fell sharply.

- Nike is 33% of Dewu footwear GMV (5.38 / 16.2, DERIVED) and Guosen states "得物平台多个季度超30%销售额来自耐克品牌" (more than 30% of Dewu sales came from Nike over several quarters).
- Nike's Dewu footwear GMV (RMB 5.38bn) exceeds Nike's entire three-platform apparel and footwear GMV that quarter (RMB 4.37bn). The Dewu gain (+RMB 1.82bn) is 2.3 times the three-platform loss (-RMB 0.80bn) (DERIVED). Adding the two (footwear on Dewu plus everything on three platforms; mismatched scope) gives Nike +11.7% YoY (DERIVED, crude).
- Guosen also states Nike's AJ1, AF1 and Dunk sales on Dewu fell over the last year, with prices rising in 2025 ("价格回升", read by Guosen as supply control). The Dewu gain therefore comes from other Nike styles at cut prices, not from the icons.
- Domestic brands on Dewu grew faster off smaller bases (Anta +121%, adidas +159%), so Dewu is not a Nike-versus-domestic divergence.
- Not separable from our data: whether Dewu Nike volume is new Nike stock diverted from official channels (which would be the "channels not aligned" Nike is closing) or consumer-to-consumer resale (which carries no Nike revenue). Guosen notes Dewu price cuts diverted sales from the three platforms ("大幅降价...分流"). Our own US resale panel (GOAT, repo `data/jordan_resale_goat_20261013.csv`) is a different market.

## 5. What 618 June 2026 implies for FY27 Q1 and Q2 China digital

- **Q1 FY27 (Jun to Aug 2026) is already reported:** GC revenue -26% c-n, digital -28%, stores -14% (10-Q). The 618 full-cycle window May 6 to Jun 21 splits 26 days into Q4 FY26 (to May 31) and 21 days into Q1 FY27 (DERIVED from window dates). The first-phase list (May 21 to 30, all in Q4 FY26) already showed Nike #3, and the full cycle did not change it, so the festival added no recovery signal to Q1.
- **GC wholesale and digital have converged:** Q1 FY27 wholesale $644M against $893M, -27.9% reported (DERIVED, data pack); digital -28% c-n. In Q1 FY26 digital was 17pp worse than total GC (-27 versus -10); in Q1 FY27 only 2pp worse (-28 versus -26). The gap that existed because partners were still growing online has closed.
- **Q2 FY27 (Sep to Nov 2026, includes 11.11):** no rank or GMV for 11.11 2026 exists yet. Evidence for direction: (1) management said on the Q1 call that the actions "will further dampen China compared to Q1 from a growth perspective for the balance of this year" (Nike management on the Q1 FY27 call, OPENED, `Q1FY27.txt`), so total GC worse than -26%; (2) the comp is easy on digital (Q2 FY26 was -36%), which partly offsets; (3) the partner clean-up date is January 1, 2027, so Q2 still contains partner online sales. We do not publish a point forecast. Arithmetic scenario only: if the Q4 2025 pattern holds (platform sales -15%, gap -20pp), the implied GC Digital for Q2 FY27 is about -35%; if the gap closes to the Q1 FY27 level of 2pp against a total GC of -30%, digital is about -32%. Both sit worse than the Q1 FY27 figure of -28%, consistent with guidance, and neither is derived from the 618 rank.
- **Exposure from the January 1 cut (Q3 FY27 onward):** Topsports-operated Nike stores on Douyin and JD carried 16.7% of Nike three-platform sales in Q4 2025 (RMB 0.73bn per quarter of GMV). If those stores go to zero and the sales are not recaptured in Nike flagships, Nike's measured platform GMV falls about 17% mechanically, which would push Nike's rank and share down on the same lists while partly moving revenue from wholesale to Direct. Recapture rate is unknowable from filings.

## 7. Smoking gun?

**No.** What this adds to the mosaic: (a) a second independent confirmation, outside Nike filings, that Nike lost share in China online (11.4% to 9.7% in one year, and out of first place on Tmall) with domestic brands also losing share (Anta 6.8% to 6.0%) and adidas flat; (b) a quantified reason why Nike's digital line is worse than the market: Nike's own Tmall flagship and the partner clean-up, not a three-times larger brand-level demand collapse; (c) a dated exposure (Topsports stores at 16.7% of platform sales before the January 1, 2027 cut). Against the short: Nike-brand platform GMV is still up 43% on three years and two-year share is down only 0.7pp (10.4% to 9.7%).
