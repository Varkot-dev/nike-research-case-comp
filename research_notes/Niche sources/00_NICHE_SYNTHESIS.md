# Niche-source hunt: what nine unusual sources found (Oct 15, 2026)

Nine Sonnet hunters (N1 to N9) tried sources the repo had not used: brand surveys, expert sell-through, Nike's own history from books and old 10-Ks, local subsidiary accounts, Chinese shopping-festival rankings, off-price retailer catalogs, amateur race shoe counts, academic papers and base rates, and the options market. The strongest candidate (N8) went to a separate skeptic (N8_check). Every note has a routes log.

## Verdict
**Still no single smoking gun.** The hunt sharpened the lead argument and added three clean, dated share-loss facts. Nothing found supports a long beyond what G7 (trademark surge) already showed.

## Repo corrections from this pass
- "Teen footwear mindshare 61% to 46%" mixes all-teen and upper-income cohorts (N1). Affected: `Nike decline root cause analysis/challengers_taste.md`, `culture_demographics.md`, `Nike decline execution vs structural/analysts_and_consumer_data.md`, `Gulden and Plank turnarounds vs Nike/nike_standing.md`, `jordan_extracts/rabbit_hole/alternative_explanations.md`, `Nike decline root cause analysis/marketing_shift.md`. Use the like-for-like upper-income series 56, 51, 45, 46 or all-teen 61 to 49.
- FY24's 12.7% is not Nike's peak EBIT margin; FY93 was 15.78%, FY21 15.54% (N3).
- "Sector loses 7.4 cents of EBIT per dollar of lost sales" is a regression slope, not a decremental margin; peer median is 31 to 40 cents (N8_check).
