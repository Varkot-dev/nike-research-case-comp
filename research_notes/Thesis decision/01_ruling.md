## Decision

**Direction: SHORT NKE (at $35.35, Oct 12, 2026).**

**One sentence:** Nike's fix is real but narrow, and at about 21x next year's earnings (65% above peers) the stock already pays for a return to better-than-FY24 margins, while Nike's own guidance shows the decline spreading and the businesses it is rebuilding earn less than the ones it is losing.

All three judges picked `short_lifestyle_structural` as the host. None picked it as written. This ruling is the repaired version.

## The three pillars

### Pillar 1. The price already pays for the recovery (the bar)
- NKE trades at 27.4x FY27 and 21.5x FY28 consensus EPS vs peer medians of 15.5x and 12.9x; adidas, growing ~9%, is 12.9x FY2. On EV/sales (1.20 vs 1.18) and EV/EBITDA (11.3 vs 11.6) Nike sits AT the peer median, so the entire premium is a margin-recovery bet. (consensus_and_valuation.md, peer table, opened today.)
- Reverse DCF, 22.5% tax, 3% revenue CAGR FY28 to FY36 (reverse_dcf.csv, opened today): implied steady-state EBIT margin 13.4% to 14.1% at 10% WACC; 11.4% to 12.2% at 9%; 9.4% to 10.3% at 8%. Compare FY26 8.3%, FY24 12.7%, 10-year mean 11.8%, and consensus-implied 5.7% (FY27) and 7.2% (FY28).
- That margin must be earned on a smaller base: FY28 consensus revenue ~$43.6B vs FY24 $51.4B.
- Presented as a grid, never as a single 13.8% headline.

### Pillar 2. The decline is spreading past the "deliberate" cuts (Nike's own numbers)
- Guide arithmetic (checked here): FY26 revenue $46,398M; Q1 FY27 $11,213M vs Q1 FY26 ~$11,720M (-4.3%). FY27 "down high single digits" (7% to 9%) leaves $31.0B to $31.9B for Q2 to Q4 vs ~$34.7B last year, i.e. -7.9% to -10.6%, worse than Q1. Advocates derive NIKE Brand ex-Greater China at -3.4% to -7.8% for Q2 to Q4 vs -0.8% in Q1.
- If Performance (~$16B, unaudited management figure) grows high-single digits, the other ~$30B must fall roughly 15% to 18%.
- Greater China is lost sell-through, not a channel choice (investigations/02): units explain -25.7 of -26 pts; GC comps compounded -12.6% while adidas grew in the same Topsports doors; Nike-owned GC stores -14%. The Jan 1, 2027 partner-online exit removes ~$743M (12.7% of GC revenue) before recapture. GC EBIT $2,309M (FY24) to $1,278M (FY26).

### Pillar 3. What replaces the lost profit earns less, and the repair keeps slipping
- NIKE Brand ASP line in the gross-margin bridge negative again (-30 bps, Q1 FY27 10-Q), 7 quarters after the worst discount quarter vs 3 to 4 quarters in Nike's own precedents.
- Clean-up end moved from "end of December 2026" (FY26 10-K p.30) to "beyond fiscal 2027" (Q1 FY27 10-Q p.20).
- Running, the growth engine, wins with discounts: Running Warehouse 49% of Nike listings marked down vs ~10% Hoka; nike.com 22 of 22 Pegasus 41 colourways discounted; same-shelf price $154.95 vs $174.95 (do NOT use $83 vs $143 ASP; that is mix).
- Demand creation 8.3% of revenue (FY24) to 11.2% (Q1 FY27); show FY26 full year 10.25% if Q1 is World Cup inflated.
- Jordan retro markdowns sit at partners (Foot Locker AJ3 21% off, AJ1 High OG 24% off vs 2 of 113 retro colourways discounted on nike.com).

## Where the judges disagreed, and how I ruled

1. **Headline framing.** PM judge: "the profit pools are shrinking and the replacement earns less" (mix). Referee: "fix too narrow and slow for 21x, decline spreading" (valuation plus guide). Risk manager: hybrid. **Ruling: lead with valuation vs earnings power (Referee), and make mix the third pillar (PM).** Reason: the guide arithmetic and the P/E table are filing and market data; the mix evidence is scraped alternative data. Hard data goes in front.
2. **Taste shift (Euromonitor, Circana, Vans/Converse, JD mix).** Referee keeps it as color; PM keeps it in. **Ruling: supporting color, not a pillar.** It is the part most like Berenberg's "irreversible share loss" Sell at $27.50, so leading with it kills differentiation.
3. **Base rates (0 of 9 recovered at EV/sales above 1.0x; Nike-like median 3-year excess -34%).** PM and risk manager make it a pillar; referee notes the +44% to +52% median 1-year rebound cuts against us. **Ruling: one slide of support, shown with the rebound statistic.** A pillar must be about Nike; base rates are about other companies.
4. **Running ASP.** **Ruling: same-shelf $154.95 vs $174.95 only**; $83 vs $143 is mix and was broken by the red team.
5. **Investor Day falsifier threshold.** PM: credible dated target of at least 13%; risk manager: at least 14% with dated steps. **Ruling: a dated long-term EBIT target of 13% or higher with an interim bridge falsifies Pillar 1.** 13% is about the bottom of the 10% WACC range (13.4%), and an undated target below ~12% supports us.
6. **Dividend.** Unanimous: catalyst and risk, not a pillar. The most likely outcome (token raise or freeze, ~85% in note 18) is a mild positive surprise against us.
