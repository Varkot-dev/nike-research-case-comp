## 1. Piper Sandler "Taking Stock With Teens": Nike's teen mindshare over time

### Takeaway
Nike is still No. 1 by a wide margin in both teen footwear and apparel, but its footwear mindshare fell from 61% (Fall 2023) to 57% (Fall 2024) to 46% (Fall 2025), with Adidas, New Balance and On gaining. Fall 2025 was the first wave where the decline was described as "stabilizing." Piper moved the survey to once a year in 2026. I found no published Fall 2026 results as of 2026-10-13.

### Cited Findings
- **Fall 2025 (published ~Oct 9, 2025):** Nike footwear mindshare **46%**, then Adidas **14%** and New Balance **8%**. On ranks No. 4 and Hoka No. 5, with no percentages given. On overtook Hoka "for the first time in the past six survey reads." Nike's footwear erosion is "stabilizing sequentially." Male footwear share grew for the first time since Fall 2022, driven by upper-income teens. Nike apparel mindshare **26%**, then Hollister 12% and Brandy Melville 6%. Teen self-reported spending fell to $2,213. [BOTH: stabilizing, but at a much lower level.], [SGB Media, Oct 9, 2025 (opened)](https://sgbonline.com/piper-sandler-teen-survey-finds-on-overtaking-hoka-in-popularity-nikes-erosion-stabilizing/); confirmed qualitatively by [Piper Sandler teen survey page (opened)](https://www.pipersandler.com/teens): "NKE remains No. 1 brand in all footwear… mindshare starting to stabilize sequentially"; "On Running (No. 4) overtook HOKA (No. 5) for the first time in six surveys"

Teen spending fell 7%., [SGI News, Oct 2025 (opened, paywalled after the lede)](https://www.sginews.com/Content/teen-survey-finds-nike-stabilizing-as-spending-falls.aspx)

### Inferences
- Approximate series for Nike footwear (all teens): Fall 2023 61% → Spring 2024 59% → Fall 2024 57% → (Spring 2025 ~49%? unverified) → Fall 2025 46%. That is roughly a 15-point loss in two years. Nike still has more than 3x the share of No. 2 Adidas.

## 2. Circana (formerly NPD) US athletic footwear data and commentary

### Takeaway
Circana's 2026 data show performance running booming (H1 2026 running dollars and units up ~13%) while sport-lifestyle declines. Nike's business is weighted toward lifestyle, but Nike is regaining ground in running. In the year to September 2025, Nike's run-specialty sales rose 35% while Hoka and On fell.

### Cited Findings
- **Run specialty, 12 months to Sept 2025** (Karnan Associates data, presented with Circana's Beth Goldstein at The Running Event, Dec 2, 2025): Nike run-specialty dollars **+35.4%**, rank moving to No. 8 from No. 9. **On −19.7%** (No. 6, down from No. 5). **Hoka −7.4%**. Brooks −6.8%. Top five brands combined −7.4%; brands 6 to 10 (On, Altra, Nike, Mizuno, Topo) +9.4%. Circana all-channel data (excluding DTC): Nike is the **third-largest** running seller with an average selling price (ASP) of **$83**; Brooks and Hoka each have ~**23%** share; On's ASP of $143 is the highest. [EXEC: Nike's return to wholesale and run specialty is working.], [SGB Media, Dec 3, 2025 (opened)](https://sgbonline.com/the-tre-sessions-run-specialty-growth-trailing-broader-market/)
- **H1 2026:** Circana Retail Tracking Service shows running shoe dollar and unit sales each up **~13%**. Performance footwear dollars **+6%**. **Sport lifestyle footwear declined.** Circana expects the US footwear industry to finish 2026 "generally flat." Goldstein says price increases pressure units. [BOTH: running tailwind for Nike's Performance segment; lifestyle (Dunk/AF1/Jordan-retro-heavy) headwind.], [WWD/FN, 2026 (snippet only; the page redirected to a paywall)](https://wwd.com/footwear-news/shoe-industry-news/consumer-spending-trends-running-shoes-1239125374/); [Circana Q1 2026 post (snippet only)](https://www.circana.com/post/us-footwear-industry-delivers-modest-first-quarter-growth-driven-by-price-increases-and-strength-in)
- On Nike's latest quarter, Goldman (Oct 2026) cited "weakness in Sportswear, Jordan, and China outweighing healthy Performance growth." This is consistent with Circana's category split., [Stocktwits/The Fly, Oct 2, 2026 (opened)](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/nke-stock-cracks-premarket-analysts-slash-targets-on-weak-outlook-citi-calls-nike-a-cost-cutting-story/cZDjOoORBKe)

### Inferences
- Circana data split the thesis. Nike's **Performance/running** recovery (fixable via product and wholesale) is real. The **sport-lifestyle category itself** is shrinking, which is a structural category-cycle headwind for Nike's largest franchises and not purely an execution failure.

### Gaps
- I found no 2026 Circana figure for Nike's overall US athletic footwear share. Circana does not publish brand shares publicly.
- I found no 2026 Matt Powell quote with Circana-type numbers. He left Circana in 2023 and is now at BCE Consulting.

## 6. Commentator opinions (former executives, industry analysts, press)

### Takeaway
Most commentators blame **self-inflicted strategic errors** under Donahoe: the DTC pivot, cutting wholesale, dissolving category teams, digital-led marketing, and overproducing hype product. That supports fixability. But most also say recovery will be slow and that Nike won't regain its former dominance, which supports a structural ceiling.

### Cited Findings
- **Massimo Giunco** (former Nike marketing executive, ~22 years; LinkedIn essay "Nike: An Epic Saga of Value Destruction," ~July 2024): traces the decline to 2020 decisions by Donahoe and Heidi O'Neill, built on three pillars: dismantling category teams (loss of product expertise), the DTC shift (strained wholesale and ceded shelf to On, Hoka, New Balance and Asics), and digital/data-driven marketing replacing brand-building ("sales activation" → discounting). He says rebuilding will take "substantial time and investment." [EXEC: self-inflicted, therefore reversible], [FashionUnited, ~Aug 2, 2024 (opened)](https://fashionunited.com/executive/report/just-cant-do-it-nikes-ongoing-strategy-struggles/2024080261226); essay framing per [Puck (snippet only)](https://puck.news/whats-the-matter-with-nike/)
- **Matt Powell** (BCE Consulting), BBC, Oct 2, 2026: Nike made "several strategic errors" that are hard to reverse: cutting retailers for DTC, over-distributing limited editions (diminishing desirability), and spending R&D on digital instead of product, which "shut down" innovation. Nike can return to growth and profitability, but innovation will not come back quickly. It will stay No. 1 but not be the "gorilla" it was. Expects Sport Offense to show positive signs next year. [BOTH], [BBC via Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 2026 (opened)](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/whats-gone-wrong-nike-worlds-205114433.html)
- **Tim Derdenger** (academic), same BBC piece: past athlete endorsements matter less; Mbappé leaving Nike for On (Sept 2026) is likened to Jordan choosing Nike, because athletes want to join "change and growth." Lamine Yamal went to Adidas to stand out from Nike's roster. [STRUCT: Nike's scale is now a disadvantage in attracting talent], same source.
- BBC data points: shares down ~75% over five years; **removed from the S&P 100** (Sept 2026; effective Sept 21, 2026 per WWD snippet); Tiger Woods left in 2024; Hill said Nike was "oversupplying" Jordan retro and will cut volume [EXEC]., same source.
- **Elliott Hill** to the FT (as relayed by Benzinga, ~Jun 2026): said he "underestimated" the work and time needed; Nike is still not delivering a consistent brand, product and marketing experience worldwide; blamed tariffs, weak demand and oil prices; expects full restructuring impact early next year. **(snippet only; FT not opened.)** [BOTH], [Benzinga, Jun 2026 (snippet)](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/06/60007561/nike-ceo-elliott-hill-admits-turnaround-is-taking-longer-than-expected-we-still-have-work-to-do)

## Summary scorecard for the thesis
- **Execution/fixable evidence:** teen mindshare "stabilizing" (Fall 2025); Nike run-specialty sales +35% while On and Hoka fell (to Sept 2025); "healthy Performance growth" (Goldman, Oct 2026); NA revenue up and wholesale mended (Fortune, Jul 2026); Jordan oversupply being cut deliberately; bulls (Bernstein, Jefferies, BTIG, Guggenheim) say "de-risked"; Giunco and Powell attribute the damage to reversible strategic choices.
- **Structural evidence:** teen footwear share down ~15 pts in two years with losses among upper-income and female teens; the sport-lifestyle category itself is declining (Circana 2026); global share −1.5 pts to 12.7% (GlobalData); China −26% and the shift to local brands; Zara overtook Nike in brand value (Kantar 2026); athletes (Mbappé, Yamal) choosing challengers; Skechers nearly level in US consideration; weak value-for-money perception; Citi's "cost-cutting story"; Williams Trading's longtime bull downgraded; the sell-side broadly questions whether FY27 is the trough.
