# Nike in China (Greater China segment): execution problem or structural problem?

Research date: 2026-10-13. Tags: **[EXEC]** = supports "execution / fixable"; **[STRUCT]** = supports "structural / not fixable"; **[BOTH]** = cuts both ways.
Source status: "opened" means I fetched and read the page. "SNIPPET ONLY" means the claim comes from a search-result summary and the page was not opened; verify it before using it in the deck.
Naming note: the brief lists "Tebu". In Chinese, 特步 (Tebu) is **Xtep**. Some Chinese articles also write **滔搏 (Topsports)**, Nike's largest distributor, and one English rendering confuses the two. These notes treat Xtep (特步) as a brand and Topsports (滔搏) as a Nike retailer.

## 1. Nike Greater China results: the headline facts (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
Nike Greater China revenue fell for nine straight quarters through Q1 FY27 (Jun–Aug 2026), and the latest quarter was the worst: -22% reported, -26% currency-neutral. Nike's own 10-K blames traffic, promotions and marketplace inventory. Nike also says the drag will last through all of FY27, so it does not expect a quick fix.

### Cited Findings
- Q1 FY27 (Jun 1–Aug 31, 2026): Greater China revenue was US$1.18B, -22% YoY (-26% currency-neutral). This was the 9th straight quarterly decline, and the drop widened after narrowing the quarter before. Global revenue was $11.21B (-4%). The stock was down 46.84% YTD. For contrast, FY2021 Greater China revenue was $8.29B (+24%), and then-CEO Donahoe said "Nike属于中国，为中国而生" ("Nike belongs to China, and was born for China"). Source: **耐克失意中国市场：大中华区业绩"九连跌"，年内股价跌近50%，新品价格频破发** ("Nike stumbles in China: Greater China down nine quarters in a row, stock down nearly 50% this year, new releases often sell below retail"), 21世纪经济报道 / 21jingji.com, republished from 蓝鲸财经, 2026-10-05 — [21jingji](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20261005/herald/e02e3ac16950b0c447cca67ae3f96e78.html) (opened). **[STRUCT/BOTH]**

- Nike's own guidance that China will drag through FY27 suggests management expects at least six more quarters of decline. That is consistent with a fix being possible but slow (see the 2027 timeline in section 4). The fact that the decline sped up in Q1 FY27 even after inventory was cleaned up weakens the claim that this is only a temporary inventory reset.

## 2. Market share trends and the Chinese brands' growth (2019–2026)

### Takeaway
Measured on a comparable revenue basis, Anta Group passed Nike China in 2022 and has kept widening the gap. Euromonitor-based shares quoted in the media disagree because they use different scopes. In 2025–26, however, Chinese brands also slowed sharply: Anta's main brand grew +3.7%, Li-Ning +3.2% and Xtep +4.2%, and in Q2 2026 Li-Ning and Xtep retail sales were negative. The market is now weak for everyone. The growth is in niche premium brands (Descente, Kolon, On, HOKA, lululemon, Salomon) and in Adidas.

### Cited Findings
- **Revenue crossover, 2022:** According to a McKinsey report cited in the press, Anta Group revenue was RMB 53.651B in 2022, surpassing Nike China's RMB 51.422B for the first time. Li-Ning was RMB 25.803B and Adidas China RMB 23.597B. — **SNIPPET ONLY** (search summary citing winshang/Jiemian, [winshang](https://m.winshang.com/news702268.html)). **[STRUCT]**
- **Euromonitor shares as quoted, with conflicting scopes:**
  - Anta Group had about 23% share in 2024 vs Nike 20.7%, Li-Ning 9.4% and Adidas 8.7% (Euromonitor, via secondary reports). — **SNIPPET ONLY**
  - A 21st Century Business Herald brand-level breakdown of sports footwear and apparel gives Nike 20.1% (2020) → 16.2% (2024) and Anta brand 8.6% → 10.5%, with Li-Ning 6.8% → 9.4%. This scope excludes FILA. — **SNIPPET ONLY**. I could not locate or open the original 21jingji article, and a Chinese search for "16.2%" found nothing.
  - An older Euromonitor figure has Anta Sports at 22.3%, the No. 1 position (the year is not stated, probably 2022–23). — **SNIPPET ONLY**

- **2025 full-year results** (all opened). Source: **从同质内卷到差异突围，中国运动品牌进入分化时代** ("From homogeneous involution to differentiated breakthroughs: China's sportswear brands enter an era of divergence"), 第一财经 Yicai, 刘晓颖, 2026-03-27 — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103107768.html):

  - Anta main brand: low single-digit growth (Q1: high single digits).
  - FILA: low single-digit growth (Q1: 10–20%).
  - Descente and Kolon: +25–30% (vs +50–55% a year earlier).
  - Li-Ning: low single-digit decline (Q1: mid single-digit growth).
  - Xtep main brand: mid single-digit decline.

  - **[STRUCT, macro]**
- **361° H1 2026:** revenue RMB 6.16B, +8%. That ends three years of growth of 18% or more. Net store count fell by 318. — [SGI Europe](https://www.sgieurope.com/financial-results/361-degrees-h1-2026-revenue-up-8/122728.article) (**SNIPPET ONLY**). **[STRUCT, macro]**

Li-Ning's 䨻 (Gamma Ray Burst) shoe ranked No. 1 for practical basketball shoes and Anta's Kyrie OVEN 1 ranked No. 2, ahead of Nike and Adidas. ### Inferences
- The best single framing for the thesis is that the mass market is in a deflationary price war at 50–70% of list price for everyone. Nike priced itself at the 699–1,099 RMB anchor, and that anchor broke. Mass domestic brands own 300–600 RMB, while specialist brands (On, HOKA, Arc'teryx, Salomon, lululemon, Descente) own the premium tier. Nike is squeezed from both sides.
- Anta Group's share gains come increasingly from its premium sub-brands (FILA, Descente, Kolon). The main Anta brand grew only +3.7% in 2025. This undercuts a pure "guochao takes the mass market" story.

- I found no 2019 baseline share figures from an opened source.

## 3. Adidas in China under Bjørn Gulden: the control test (macro vs Nike-specific)

### Takeaway
Adidas Greater China grew double digits (currency-neutral) through 2025 and H1 2026, during the same quarters Nike fell 10–26%. This is the strongest single piece of evidence that a Western brand can grow in today's China. It supports the view that Nike's decline is largely self-inflicted.

### Cited Findings
- **Q1 2026:** Adidas Greater China +17% to €1.14B (about $1.34B), its 12th consecutive quarter of growth. — [Jing Daily, 2026-04-30](https://jingdaily.com/intels/2026-04/30/adidas-posts-14-revenue-growth-in-q1-2026) (opened). **[EXEC]**
- **Q2 2026:** Greater China +15% currency-neutral and H1 +16%, attributed to "continued strong market share gains". — [adidas press release](https://www.adidas-group.com/en/media/press-releases/adidas-grows-top-line-14percent-and-achieves-record-sales-in-q2) (**SNIPPET ONLY**; the page returned 503). **[EXEC]**
- **Full-year 2025:** Adidas China sales +13% to about US$4.2B. Gulden named outdoor and street dance as new growth drivers and said Adidas will keep opening stores and building e-commerce in China. — [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3370520/adidas-ceo-taps-outdoor-sport-street-dance-new-drivers-china-competition-heightens) (**SNIPPET ONLY**; the page returned 403). **[EXEC]**
- Rough comparison: Adidas China is about $4.2B (CY2025) and growing, vs Nike Greater China at $5.85B (FY26) and falling. At these trajectories the gap is closing fast.
- Chinese media describe Adidas as concentrating resources on local retail operations and event marketing — [21jingji 2026-10-05](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20261005/herald/e02e3ac16950b0c447cca67ae3f96e78.html) (opened).
- Two runners finished under 2 hours at the 2026 London Marathon in Adidas shoes, and the Samba is having a fashion revival. The article contrasts this with Kipchoge's Nike-shod sub-2 run in 2019. — [Sina Finance 2026-06-05](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-06-05/doc-iniaikax1918825.shtml) (opened). **[EXEC]**: product and marketing leadership can be won back.

### Inferences
- Adidas, On, HOKA and lululemon are all growing in China during a consumer slump. That suggests macro conditions explain only part of Nike's decline. The domestic mass brands' slowdown shows a macro headwind exists, but it is single-digit, not -20%+.
- Caveat for the structural side: Adidas is coming off a low base. Its China business was crushed in 2021–22 after the Xinjiang boycott and its own inventory mess, so part of its growth is recovery. Nike's peak was higher, so it has more share to lose in a price war.

## 4. Discounting, inventory, resale (Dewu 得物) and the January 2027 channel change

### Takeaway
Nike's price integrity in China has collapsed. Shoes sell at 50–70% of list in outlets, and recent Jordan retros trade on Dewu at roughly 53–88% of retail. Nike's main fix is to end online sales through distributors such as Topsports and Pou Sheng from 2027-01-01 and run digital itself. That is a controllable execution lever, but it carries a large short-term revenue risk, and results are not expected before late 2027.

### Cited Findings
- **Dewu (得物) average resale prices**, all below retail (破发) — [21jingji 2026-10-05](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20261005/herald/e02e3ac16950b0c447cca67ae3f96e78.html) (opened). **[EXEC/BOTH]**
  - Air Jordan 11 Retro "University Blue" (spring 2026): retail ¥1,399, average sale ¥764.73 (about 55% of retail).
  - Air Jordan 4 Retro "Comic" (summer 2026): retail ¥1,599, average ¥848.49 (about 53%).
  - Jordan × J Balvin AJ4 (autumn 2026): retail ¥1,599, average ¥1,404.
  - In 2021, flipping a popular AJ earned about ¥500.
- **Dunk resale collapse:** many former hype Dunks now trade below retail on Dewu and StockX. One white/grey-blue Dunk retailing at ¥699 once resold for over ¥1,500. The article also claims Dunk revenue fell "nearly 50%". The article gives no data source, so this is commentary only. Source: **【BT财报瞬析】连续九个季度暴跌！耐克大中华区的"失去"** ("[BT Earnings Flash] Nine straight quarters of decline! ### Inferences
- Every lever Nike is pulling is an execution lever: inventory cleanup, fewer promotions, cutting distributors, local design and a new GM. This supports the "fixable" thesis. However, (a) the decline sped up after the inventory cleanup, (b) the channel cut will mechanically lower FY27 and FY28 revenue, and (c) the payoff is dated to late 2027. A case team should present this as "fixable in principle, but the timeline runs well past the current window."
- Below-retail Dewu prices on new Jordan retros are a direct read on brand heat. As long as new releases sell below retail, full-price sell-through cannot recover.

## 5. Guochao (国潮) and the 2021 Xinjiang cotton boycott: size and persistence

### Takeaway
The 2021 Xinjiang boycott caused a sharp, measurable short-term collapse in Nike and Adidas online sales. But Adidas, the harder-hit brand, now grows double digits in China, and Nike's revenue reached its peak after the boycott ($8.29B in FY21). That evidence suggests nationalism alone is not a permanent barrier. Guochao preference is strong in surveys, but in 2025–26 the domestic mass brands themselves are stalling, so guochao is not translating into growth for them either.

### Cited Findings
- **April 2021 boycott damage:** Morningstar's Ivan Su estimated Tmall sales fell 78% YoY for Adidas and 59% for Nike, while Li-Ning's Tmall sales rose more than ninefold. Overall sportswear sales on Tmall fell 11%. The boycott was triggered by a People's Daily post after Western sanctions over Xinjiang. — **SNIPPET ONLY** ([Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.nl/nike-and-adidas-saw-their-online-sales-plummet-in-china-over-the-xinjiang-cotton-boycott/), which redirected; [SCMP 2021](https://m.scmp.com/business/companies/article/3127107/anta-li-ning-shares-surge-chinese-consumers-back-domestic-brands)). **[STRUCT, date: 2021]**
- **Persistence counter-evidence:**
  - Nike Greater China FY2021 revenue was $8.29B (+24%), the fiscal year that included the boycott — [21jingji 2026-10-05](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20261005/herald/e02e3ac16950b0c447cca67ae3f96e78.html) (opened).
  - Adidas, the worst-hit brand in 2021, has now had 12+ straight growth quarters — [Jing Daily](https://jingdaily.com/intels/2026-04/30/adidas-posts-14-revenue-growth-in-q1-2026) (opened).
  - **[EXEC]**
- **Guochao size:**
  - A Statista survey (March 2024), reported by RetailBoss, found 89% of Chinese consumers prefer domestic fashion brands over foreign ones. This covers fashion broadly, not sportswear. — **SNIPPET ONLY** ([RetailBoss](https://retailboss.co/89-percent-of-chinese-shoppers-preferred-homegrown-brands-over-foreign-brands-fashion-study)).
  - PwC (2021): 37% of Chinese respondents said they were more inclined than six months earlier to buy domestic brands. — **SNIPPET ONLY**
  - Rakuten Insight (Aug 2025): Nike was still the most-purchased sportswear brand in China, with 64% of respondents reporting a purchase. — **SNIPPET ONLY** ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/372774/chinese-penetration-rate-of-sportswear-brands/)).
  - **[BOTH]**
- **Change in what consumers want:** consumers have moved from "品牌崇拜" (brand worship) to "自我表达" (self-expression), with demand fragmenting into running, trail, hiking, commuting, gym and frisbee. The article argues consumers "并非没钱，也不是不愿花钱" ("are neither short of money nor unwilling to spend") but won't pay full price for a brand losing its distinctiveness. — [Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-06-05/doc-iniaikax1918825.shtml) (opened). **[BOTH]**: the change is structural, but the cure is a product and assortment choice Nike controls.
- **Chinese academic papers (all SNIPPET ONLY; the CNKI-style index pages returned 500 or were not opened):**
  - 《上海经济》 *Shanghai Economy*, 2024, on intention and behaviour toward domestic products (国货消费意向与行为): identity, value and belonging drive *intention*, but perceived usefulness and function-for-price drive *actual purchase*. — [napstic index](https://search.napstic.cn/literature/periodical/010shjj202404006). **[EXEC]**: patriotic intention does not automatically convert into sales, so a product that wins on value can still compete.
  - 《毛纺科技》 *Wool Textile Journal*, 2024, Vol. 52(7): 69–76, DOI 10.19333/j.mfkj.20230506108. In a survey of 241 respondents, perceived emotional, cognitive and experiential value mediate between traditional-culture advertising and guochao purchase intention. — [napstic](https://search.napstic.cn/literature/periodical/010mfkj202407010).
  - 《丝绸》 *Silk*, 2026 No. 6, on how brand attributes affect guochao apparel purchase intention (fsQCA + SEM), DOI 10.3969/j.issn.1001-7003.2026.06.003.
  - Iowa State University thesis (2017) on country-of-origin effects in celebrity endorsements in China's sportswear market — [ISU](https://dr.lib.iastate.edu/handle/20.500.12876/51798).
  - A 2024 paper in the *International Journal of Physical Activity and Health* reports Chinese preference shifting from foreign to domestic sportswear brands — [Boise State ScholarWorks](https://scholarworks.boisestate.edu/ijpah/vol3/iss3/93).

### Inferences
- Guochao is a lasting floor that raises the bar for foreign brands, which is **[STRUCT]**. The evidence that it explains Nike's 2024–26 collapse is weak, however, because Adidas, lululemon, On and HOKA are growing in the same environment. A fair conclusion: geopolitics and guochao are a tailwind for Chinese brands and a ceiling on Nike's former roughly 20% share, but they do not cause -20% quarters.
- A geopolitical tail risk remains, separately from guochao: US–China tariff or sanction flare-ups could trigger a new boycott. This is a structural risk Hill's team cannot control.

### Gaps

## 6. Synthesis: scorecard of execution vs structural evidence

### Takeaway
On balance, the evidence leans toward an execution problem made much worse by a structural shift. The case for "fixable" is the Adidas comparison plus the fact that every cited cause is a Nike lever: discounting, inventory, a messy channel, a global assortment, and lost running credibility. The case for "not fixable on the thesis timeline" rests on four points: the whole market is in a deflationary price war; the 2020–2024 share loss looks permanent; Nike's own guidance has China dragging through FY27; and the channel reset lowers revenue before it raises it.
