# Hypothesis: US Hispanic and urban sneaker consumers pulled back in 2025 to 2026 under immigration enforcement

Date: 2026-10-13. Status labels: OPENED = I read the page or filing myself in this session. SNIPPET = search-result summary only. REPO = figure already verified in this repo (data/nike_filing_facts.csv, research_notes/04_financial_data_pack.md). Arithmetic is mine and marked "my arithmetic".

Effort note: the hypothesis was flagged low-to-moderate confidence going in. One test (Nike NA revenue by quarter vs enforcement timing) falsifies it as a material driver of Nike's decline, so I limited the work to that test plus the cleanest natural experiment I could find (Foot Locker's WSS banner). I did not buy Placer.ai or Numerator data.

## Bottom line

- **The consumer effect is real at the door level.** Foot Locker's Hispanic-focused WSS banner comped -8.1% in Feb to Jul 2025's second quarter while Foot Locker, Champs and Kids Foot Locker comped +1.8%, +2.0% and +7.6% in the same quarter. Foot Locker blamed "soft store traffic trends, particularly in our WSS and international businesses" (Foot Locker Q2 2025 8-K, OPENED). JD's CEO tied a "huge decline in traffic" at Shoe Palace (Feb to Apr 2025) to immigration policy (Reuters via MarketScreener, Nov 12, 2025, OPENED). Kantar: Hispanic self-reported visits to mass retailers -14.7% YoY in Apr to Jun 2025 vs -4.5% for non-Hispanic shoppers (same Reuters piece, OPENED).
- **But WSS was already underperforming before enforcement.** WSS trailed Foot Locker North America by 3 to 8 points in 2023 and 2024. 2025 widened the gap by only about 2 points on average (my arithmetic, table below).
- **It does not show up in Nike's numbers.** Nike North America fell in every quarter from Q1 FY25 (Jun to Aug 2024), before the enforcement surge. It then grew in every quarter during the peak enforcement period: +4.0%, +8.8%, +3.3%, +2.7%, +2.1% (Q1 FY26 to Q1 FY27, REPO, my arithmetic). The lifestyle weakness Nike describes is global (Greater China -26%, EMEA -5% in Q1 FY27), so it is not a US Hispanic story.
- **Verdict: minor.** Plausible size is about $0.1B to $0.4B a year of Nike NA revenue at most. That is about 2% to 6% of the $4.96B FY24 to FY26 revenue decline, and probably the low end, because the timing barely overlaps. Management cannot fix the cause (federal policy). It can only route around it through channel mix, which it is already doing (Dick's, Academy, Amazon).

## Sizing (my arithmetic, assumptions stated)

- Exposure: Hispanics were about 23% of US sneaker sales in 2016 (NPD via NBC, OPENED). Assume 20% to 28% of Nike NA consumer revenue today. No current figure found.
- Shock: Numerator, Hispanic household spending growth 0.8% in H1 2025 vs 3.3% for white households (SNIPPET via search summary; Bloomberg/BGOV excerpt Sept 6, 2025, OPENED in prior pass). Discretionary categories fell faster (Circana, SNIPPET). WSS relative gap widened about 2 to 5 points. Assume a 2% to 8% relative drop in Hispanic sneaker spend.
- Nike NA FY26 revenue $20,511M (REPO). Hit = 20,511 x 0.20 to 0.28 x 0.02 to 0.08 = about $80M to $460M a year, central about $0.2B.
- Against the FY24 to FY26 decline of $4,964M (REPO) that is about 2% to 9% at the extreme, but only part of a year overlaps (Feb to May 2025 in FY25; FY26 grew in NA). Realistic share: about 2% to 4%, labelled low confidence.
- For FY27: Q1 FY27 NA still +2%. The FY27 high-single-digit guide is driven by Greater China, Converse and the Jordan reset (REPO, 04_financial_data_pack.md). A Hispanic pullback could be a small part of Jordan and lifestyle softness in US urban doors, but Jordan's decline is global (Jordan 13% of revenue, falling mid-teens, Q1 FY27 call, OPENED).

## Strongest evidence for

1. Foot Locker Q2 2025: WSS -8.1% vs Kids Foot Locker +7.6% in the same quarter, and management singled out WSS traffic (8-K filed Aug 27, 2025, OPENED).
2. Shoe Palace traffic drop Feb to Apr 2025, explicitly tied to immigration policy by JD's CEO (Reuters, Nov 12, 2025, OPENED via MarketScreener).
3. Kantar: Hispanic mass-retail visits -14.7% vs -4.5% for others, Apr to Jun 2025 (same Reuters piece, OPENED).
4. Placer.ai: LA Fashion District visits fell from 386,000 to 246,000 week on week after the June 2025 raids; Jan to Jun 2026 still 9.9% below 2024 (Bisnow, SNIPPET). UCLA (July 2026): 46,000 fewer visits near nine raid sites in two weeks (SNIPPET).
5. Precedent: NPD 2017 high-teens drop in Hispanic sneaker purchases (NBC, OPENED).

## Strongest evidence against

1. Nike NA grew in every quarter from Q1 FY26 to Q1 FY27 (+4.0%, +8.8%, +3.3%, +2.7%, +2.1%), exactly the peak enforcement window (REPO).
2. Nike NA's decline started in Q1 FY25 (Jun to Aug 2024, -11.0%), before the cause existed (REPO).
3. WSS underperformed Foot Locker NA by 3 to 8 points throughout 2023 and 2024, so most of its weakness is not enforcement (Foot Locker 8-Ks, OPENED).
4. Lifestyle weakness is global: Greater China -26%, EMEA -5% in Q1 FY27; Hill's "lack of energy in the lifestyle space" is a global remark (Q1 FY27 call, OPENED via Webull).
5. Nike never cited it; Hill reported good NA sell-through "across all channels" including JD/Foot Locker (Q1 FY27 call, OPENED).
6. JD's 2026 NA weakness (-6.8% LFL to Aug 1, 2026) is blamed on sentiment, high-heat product and back-to-school timing, not immigration (World Footwear, OPENED).

## Independent cause or symptom?

Independent and exogenous at the consumer level (federal enforcement policy), layered on a broader lower-income squeeze. For Nike it is a channel-exposure issue: it hurts Hispanic-skewed doors (WSS, Shoe Palace, some Hibbett and DTLR) that are a small share of Nike NA, while Nike's NA growth in FY26 came from big-box and new wholesale accounts. It does not explain the FY24 to FY26 decline, which the companion notes attribute to product, franchise oversupply and the DTC pivot.

## What would change the verdict

- Placer.ai visit data for WSS, Shoe Palace and Hibbett doors in Hispanic-majority ZIP codes vs Dick's and Academy doors in the same ZIPs, monthly 2024 to 2026.
- Circana athletic footwear sales by ethnicity, 2024 to 2026.
- Nike NA lifestyle vs performance split by channel. Not disclosed.
