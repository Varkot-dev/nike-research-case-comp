## Headline findings
- CFO is no longer Matt Friend. David Denton (ex-Pfizer, Lowe's, CVS) became CFO on Aug 17, 2026 (announced Jun 23, 2026). Friend stayed through Sep 4, 2026. https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-inc-announces-planned-cfo-transition
- A new Chief Commercial Officer, Jane Ewing (ex-Walmart), started Sep 7, 2026 (announced Aug 27, 2026). The earlier CCO role (Craig Williams) was eliminated in Dec 2025. https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-inc-august-27-2026-senior-leadership-announcement
- Dec 2025 reorg: COO role created for Venkatesh Alagirisamy (effective Dec 8, 2025); CTO (Muge Dogan) and CCO (Craig Williams) roles eliminated; four geography heads added to the SLT reporting to Hill. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018725000115/sltannouncement.htm
- Pattern under Hill: mostly insiders and returnees in product, brand and regions; outside hires only in finance (Denton) and commercial (Ewing), both from non-sport consumer/retail sectors.

## Elliott Hill, President and CEO (the hirer)
- Career: Joined Nike Jun 1, 1988 (Ohio University campus recruiting; earlier Dallas Cowboys athletic training work, sources vary). Sports Graphics Sales 1989, Team Sales 1991, Director Team Sports Division 1998, President of Consumer and Marketplace 2018. Retired 2020 after about 32 years. Post-retirement: boards (Weber, TCU) and senior advisor at BDT & MSD Partners. Named CEO Sep 19, 2024; started Oct 14, 2024, succeeding John Donahoe. https://www.cnbc.com/2024/10/01/nikes-new-ceo-started-as-an-intern-and-worked-his-way-up.html and https://www.modaes.com/global/profiles/elliott-hill
- Trajectory: lifer, retiree, then returnee CEO. Reversed Donahoe's gender-based product structure back toward sport-based teams ("Sport Offense"; "Win Now" plan). https://fortune.com/article/nike-ceo-veteran-elliott-hill-turnaround-growth/
- Confidence: HIGH on dates; MED on education details (sources conflict).
- Not verified: Mark Parker's current title (Executive Chairman). Training knowledge says yes but I did not confirm it in this session.
