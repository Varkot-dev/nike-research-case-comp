## Verdict
**No smoking gun.** After 30 hunts across three waves (G1 to G12, N1 to N9, W1 to W9) plus seven deep dives, free data does not contain a single fact that decides long or short. Wave 3 found that the Street has already cut FY28 EPS close to our filing-based bear case, which moves the whole argument onto the long-run margin, and found two facts that shape Investor Day odds.

## What this means for the pitch
1. **Lead with the margin, not FY28 EPS.** W7 shows the Street has already done most of the EPS cut. The durable short argument is N8: the price needs about 11% to 14% EBIT margin by FY31, base rates say about 9%, about one in five odds of 11.6%. W9 adds why: demand creation alone is 216 bp above FY24 and contractually sticky.
2. **Investor Day odds lean to the short but the market already prices a symmetric move.** W2 lowers the chance of a raised dated target; N9 shows options price only about 6% odds of a 10% drop. A put spread is the cleanest expression.
3. **Drop channel stuffing entirely.** D2, G2 and W6 now all say the wholesale surge is channel mix, retailer consolidation and a refill of the FY25 destock.
4. **Near-term catalysts before submission:** Topsports Jun to Aug update (about Oct 21 to 22) and interim results (Oct 28); Feng Tay October revenue (about Nov 10); Pou Sheng October (about Nov 10).
