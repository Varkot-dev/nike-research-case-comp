# Crux: Is North America end-consumer demand for Nike growing, or is NA wholesale growth channel fill?

Prepared 2026-10-14. Labels: OPENED = I fetched and read the page or filing in this session. OPENED (repo) = read by an earlier researcher, recorded in this repo, not re-fetched. SNIPPET = search-result summary only. [my arithmetic] = computed by me.

## 0. Pre-registered decision rules (written before gathering new data)

Proxy for NA consumer demand for Nike = Nike NA Direct (Nike's own sell-through) plus partner sell-through of Nike (retailer comps weighted by Nike share, retailer Nike-specific commentary, POS and card-spend panels).

Points LONG (genuine sell-through growth):
- L1. Nike-heavy retailers (Dick's, Academy, JD/Hibbett, Famous Footwear, Shoe Carnival, Genesco/Journeys) report positive athletic footwear comps in their quarters covering May to Aug 2026, and name Nike as a driver.
- L2. Their inventory grows no faster than sales (inventory/sales flat or down), with stable or rising merchandise margins (no markdown clearing).
- L3. Third-party POS or card data (Circana, Bank of America, Earnest, Consumer Edge, Placer.ai) show Nike US sales flat-to-up in 2026.
- L4. Government series (Census shoe-store sales, BLS footwear CPI) show category demand growing in real terms in 2026.
- L5. Implied Nike NA consumer sell-through (Direct plus partner sell-through) grows at least low-single digits, so the +9% wholesale gap is explained mostly by share shift from Direct to wholesale rather than fill.

Points SHORT (channel fill that unwinds):
- S1. Nike-heavy retailers comp negative in athletic footwear, or name Nike/Jordan as a drag, while their inventory grows faster than sales.
- S2. Retailers report rising markdowns or aggressive promotions in Nike product; Nike product discounted on retailer sites.
- S3. POS or card data show Nike US consumer sales down in 2026 while Nike NA wholesale is +9%.
- S4. Retailers guide to lower Nike receipts or "cleaner" inventory in H2 2026 (order cuts coming).
- S5. Implied consumer sell-through flat or down: the gap between wholesale (+9%) and consumer demand (about 0%) is fill.

Neutral or unresolved: retailer data mixed by channel (sporting goods up, mall athletic down) with total consumer sell-through between 0% and +2%, and no POS data.

## 1. Verdict

**Points SHORT on this crux (strength: strong, not decisive).** End-consumer demand for Nike in North America was **not growing** in May to August 2026. On my estimate it was roughly **-3% to +1% in dollars (central about -1%)** in Nike's Q1 FY27 (Jun to Aug 2026). In units or real terms it was lower, because footwear prices were up about 3.5% [my arithmetic, section 4]. Over the same quarter Nike reported NA wholesale +9% and NA total +2% (Q1 FY27 10-Q, North America segment discussion).

The gap between shipments and sell-through has four parts:
- one genuine new door (Amazon, from fall 2025);
- price and mix;
- a one-off World Cup sell-in;
- an inventory build concentrated at Foot Locker.

Nike, Dick's, JD, Shoe Carnival and Caleres all describe that build as excess aged lifestyle product that is now being promoted and cut from order books.

The pre-registered tests scored as follows:
- Held: S1, S2, S3 (partial: Circana category data only, no Nike-specific POS), S4 and S5.
- Failed: L1 (except at Dick's), L2 (except at Dick's and Academy), L3, L4 and L5.

Why "strong" and not "decisive": no public Nike-specific POS or card panel exists, so the Nike share of each retailer's comp is inferred, not measured.

What it means for the pitch:
- The NA wholesale growth that bulls point to is not proof of consumer demand.
- The unwind is already under way and partly in guidance: about 400 bps Q2 FY27 headwind, FY27 revenue down high single digits, order books cut.
- A SHORT therefore needs this crux plus evidence that the unwind runs deeper or longer than guided. That second part is not settled here.
- Partners report an improving trend for August and September 2026 (section 3.7). That is the main risk to the SHORT reading.

### T2. Nike's own statements, Q1 FY27 call (Oct 1, 2026; Nike IR transcript PDF, OPENED)

- Hill: "some aged, higher volume, sportswear footwear sold through below expectations. Looking ahead, that has impacted our future order books as we proactively work with our wholesale partners to work through excess inventory" (PDF p. 3 to 4 area).
- Hill: "we've been oversupplying our iconic retro product ... In the near term, North America will feel the biggest impact."
- Friend: about 400 bps Q2 FY27 headwind from EMEA Cyber Week and "a higher comparison of North America sell-in activities versus last year."
- Hill: "we are seeing good sell-through across all channels ... performance product is selling" at DICK'S, Academy, SCHEELS, JD, Foot Locker.
- Q1 FY27 10-Q, NA segment: wholesale +9%; NIKE Direct -6% (digital -6%, stores -6%); footwear units +2% with "higher discounts" lowering ASP. Unlike the FY26 10-K, it gives no driver for the wholesale increase (Q1 FY27 10-Q, OPENED (scratchpad copy)).
- FY26 10-K: NA wholesale +14% "including expanded distribution, higher shipments to existing partners and fewer marketplace management actions" (FY26 10-K p. 37, OPENED (repo)). "Higher shipments to existing partners" coincides with negative comps at Foot Locker, JD NA, Famous and Shoe Carnival.

### T3. Retailer statements on channel inventory and promotion (Aug to Sep 2026)

- Stack (DKS call, Aug 25, 2026): "The industry is carrying too much inventory"; "I've never seen the specialty channel of distribution so promotional in my career"; legacy silhouettes "to remain challenging at least through the fourth quarter"; but "I don't think this is a demand issue going forward from a footwear standpoint" and footwear "is going to comp positively in the back half". Orders: "modifying some orders here and there, but nothing really out of the ordinary." (Motley Fool transcript, OPENED)
- Shoe Station: "a footwear marketplace that became increasingly promotional as the quarter progressed"; "promotional environment will persist through the balance of the year" (Sep 10, 2026 release, OPENED).
- JD: headwinds "may persist into H2: ... ongoing product cycle evolution in footwear at some of our major brand partners, and a promotional market backdrop" (HY27 results, OPENED).
- Caleres: Famous pivoting "to quickly align our assortment ... emphasizing fashion" away from lifestyle athletic (Sep 9, 2026 release, OPENED).

## 3. Analysis

### 3.1 Partner-weighted Nike sell-through, May to Jul 2026 [my arithmetic; weights and Nike-specific rates are estimates]

| Partner | Est. annual Nike NA wholesale ($B) | Nike sell-through range (low / mid / high) | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| DICK'S Business | 2.1 | 0 / +3 / +6% | Comp +4.9% incl. World Cup (adidas-led per Hobart); Nike running strong, legacy weak |
| Foot Locker NA | 1.95 (repo range 1.8 to 2.1) | -8 / -6 / -4% | NA comp about -3.7%; Nike-heavy legacy and launch product named as the problem |
| JD NA (JD, Finish Line, Hibbett, DTLR, Shoe Palace) | 1.5 | -9 / -7 / -5% | Footwear organic c.-5% H1, Q2 LFL -6.8% |
| Academy | 0.5 | -1 / 0 / +2% | Footwear -1%, Nike running named a driver |
| Famous Footwear + Shoe Carnival | 0.4 | -10 / -6 / -2% | Famous comp -5.9% led by lifestyle athletic; SHOE athletics -2.2% |
| **Tracked total** | **6.45 (54% of FY26 NA wholesale of $11,958M)** | **-5.2% / -2.8% / -0.4%** | about -$6M to -$84M per quarter |

Shipment growth to these same partners was positive: Nike NA wholesale was +9% in total, and the FY26 10-K names "higher shipments to existing partners". Sell-in therefore exceeded sell-through at the tracked half of the wholesale book.

### 3.2 Nike NA consumer-facing demand, Q1 FY27 [my arithmetic, estimates]

| Component | Q1 FY27 YoY $M |
|---|---|
| NIKE Direct NA | -138 (Q1 FY27 10-Q; repo T1) |
| Tracked partners' Nike sell-through | -84 to -6 |
| Amazon brand store (new door since fall 2025, not yet lapped) | +75 to +125 (Goldman estimate of $300M to $500M a year, SNIPPET) |
| Untracked partners (Scheels, run specialty, department stores, off-price, Canada; about $5.5B a year) | 0% to +5% growth, i.e. 0 to +69 |

The net is about -$145M to +$46M on a Q1 FY26 NA base of $5,020M, i.e. **-2.9% to +0.9%**. Reported NA revenue was +2.1%, and NA wholesale was +9.0% (+$245M).

### 3.3 Where the extra shipments went

The main inventory build in the tracked channel is Foot Locker:
- Foot Locker Business inventory +$250M year on year at Aug 1, 2026, with sales -6.5% (repo T4).
- About 59% of that is Nike by purchase share, and about 70% of Foot Locker sales are NA. That gives about $100M of Nike product at NA cost [my arithmetic, estimate].

Elsewhere, inventory is flat or lean:
- DICK'S +6% on sales +5.6%.
- Academy per store -5.6% in units.
- SHOE -5%.
- Caleres ex Stuart Weitzman -1.2%.
- JD group +1.8%.

Nike's own NA inventory was -0.9% (Q1 FY27 10-Q p. 17, repo). Inventory therefore moved from Nike's warehouses to Foot Locker.

## 4. Alternatives tested

| Alternative explanation for wholesale growing faster than sell-through | Status | Data |
|---|---|---|
| New doors (Amazon, Academy Jordan, run specialty) are genuine sell-through that comps do not capture | **Partly true, does not close the gap** | Even at the high Amazon estimate and +5% at untracked accounts, consumer demand is about +0.9%, not +9%. Amazon laps from Q2 FY27. |
| Price, not units | **Partly true** | Footwear CPI +3.4% to +5.2%; NA footwear units +2% with ASP down on "higher discounts". Real demand is lower than the dollar figure, not higher. |
| World Cup sell-in that sold through | **True but one-off** | DKS comp "does include World Cup", led by adidas marketing; JD notes replica kit outside NA. Laps from Q2 FY27. |
| Macro consumer weakness, not Nike-specific | **Ruled out as the main cause** | Boot Barn comp +4.7%, Journeys +2%, sporting goods stores +7% to +13%, running +13% (Circana). Weakness is concentrated in lifestyle athletic, where Nike is the largest brand. |
| Retailer-specific problems (Foot Locker integration, Finish Line conversion, Famous assortment) | **Partly true, not sufficient** | The weakness repeats across five unrelated operators, and all name lifestyle athletic, end-of-cycle product or legacy silhouettes. JD ex-Finish Line NA comparable is still -2.5%. |
| Back-to-school timing shift into August (comps understate demand) | **Partly true; the main remaining risk** | SHOE August comp -2.7% (athletic adult up low single digits); Famous flat quarter to date through Labor Day; Boot Barn +2%; JD says BTS deferred to August. Improvement, not growth. |
| Sporting goods strength means Nike demand is strong | **Ruled out** | Category-wide series; Academy footwear -1%; DKS growth partly World Cup and trading cards. |
| Foot Locker reclassified into the Census sporting goods series | **Ruled out** | Shoe stores would have fallen about 14%; they did not. |

## 5. What remains unknown, and what would settle it

- **Nike-specific POS.** The Circana brand-level numbers exist but are paid. Any Circana or card-panel citation of Nike US sales for Jun to Sep 2026 would replace the inferred weights in 3.1.
- **August to October 2026 sell-through.**
  - Census shoe-store sales for August and September (MRTS release mid-October and mid-November 2026).
  - Retailer Q3 results: Academy, DKS and Foot Locker in late November; SHOE and Caleres in early December. These come after the Nov 1 submission but before the Nov 19 finals.
  - Nike Investor Day (Nov 16 to 17).
- **Nike Q2 FY27 NA wholesale (late December).** If it falls more than guided while Dick's comps stay positive, the fill is unwinding faster than guidance, which points to a deeper SHORT.
