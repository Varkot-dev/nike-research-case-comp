# Crux: has Nike's Greater China decline found a floor?

Research date: 2026-10-14. Builds on (does not redo): `Nike decline root cause analysis/china.md` (stoic-shannon branch) and `investigations/02_greater_china_decline.md` (zealous-mccarthy branch).

Labels: OPENED = I read the primary document text myself today. SNIPPET = search-result summary only. REPO = taken from an earlier repo note, with the status that note recorded. "My arithmetic" = simple arithmetic on OPENED figures.

## 0. Pre-registered test (written before gathering data)

- **Points LONG (floor):** in the newest periods, Topsports and Pou Sheng declines narrow quarter over quarter. Nike's own guidance implies Q2 to Q4 FY27 China declines that are better than Q1's -26%. NBS sporting goods sales stabilise. adidas and Anta keep growing, but Nike's gap to them shrinks. Nike holds or regains its Tmall 618 and Double 11 rank, and Dewu resale prices on new releases move back toward retail.
- **Points SHORT (still deepening):** Topsports and Pou Sheng declines are flat or getting wider. Management guides China worse than -26%. A structural cut to channel volume is still ahead. Nike's Tmall rank slips again, and new Jordan releases trade far below retail on Dewu.

## 1. Answer

**SHORT, strong.** Reported Greater China revenue has not found a floor and is guided to get worse through H2 FY27:

1. **Management guides China worse than Q1's -26%.** On the Q1 FY27 call (OPENED), Denton said: "the guidance range that I just provided to you assumes that China actually gets worse from a revenue perspective for the balance of this year." He also said: "the actions that we're taking in China will further dampen China compared to Q1 from a growth perspective for the balance of this year."
2. **A large channel cut is still ahead.** All partner online sales of Nike in mainland China end on 2027-01-01, which is in Nike's Q3 FY27. Nike online sales were about 22% of Topsports' revenue and about 15% of Pou Sheng's (HKEX filings, 2026-07-22, REPO and OPENED in prior notes).
3. **The largest partner's trend was still widening at its last report.** Topsports went from a mid-single-digit decline, to high-single-digit, to high-single-digit, to low-single-digit, and then to a low-teens decline in Mar to May 2026 (OPENED).

There is one genuine early sign of a floor in consumer sell-through: Pou Sheng's September 2026 revenue fell only 2.5%. That is a single month, it is helped by the calendar, and Pou Sheng sells many brands, not only Nike. It does not offset items 1 to 3 for H2 FY27 reported revenue.

## 3. Alternative explanations tested

1. **"Pou Sheng's September -2.5% is the floor."**
   - Calendar: Mid-Autumn Festival fell on 2026-09-25, against 2025-10-06. Some holiday spending shifts into September 2026, and September 2025 was depressed by the reverse shift (it was -4.8% against a 2024 Mid-Autumn of Sept 17).
   - Mix: about 15% of Pou Sheng revenue is Nike online, which ends 2026-12-31, so partners may be clearing that stock now and lifting sales.
   - Multi-brand: Pou Sheng sells many brands.
   - **Not ruled out.** September has the best 2-year stack since May (-7.2%), but it is one month. October data (due about 2026-11-10) avoids the holiday shift because National Day falls Oct 1 to 8 in both years.
2. **"Nike's Q1 FY27 drop was just the market."**
   - The market did slow in Q2 2026: every peer decelerated by 5 to 15 points, and NBS sporting goods ran from -2% to -11%.
   - But Nike's gap to adidas widened from about -27 to about -41 points, and Nike's Tmall rank fell while adidas rose to #1.
   - **Ruled out as the full explanation.** The market explains at most about 5 to 8 of the 26 points.
3. **"The decline is just deliberate destocking, and sell-through is fine."**
   - Partner sell-through is also falling: Topsports was low-teens down for Mar to May 2026, and implied Nike sales at Pou Sheng were -18% to -30% for Jun to Aug (REPO).
   - New Jordans sell for 53% to 55% of retail on Dewu.
   - **Largely ruled out.** Destocking adds to the decline, but consumer weakness is real.
4. **"Tmall rank is stable at #3, so share has stabilised."**
   - Rank is ordinal and confounded by Nike's deliberate promotion cuts.
   - **Unresolved.** Settling it needs GMV values, not ranks (for example from 久谦中台 or 魔镜 data).
5. **"Even if reported revenue deepens, the market already knows."**
   - True for the guidance, which is public.
   - **This is a valuation question, not a floor question,** and it is outside this crux.

## 4. What is still unknown, and what would settle it

- **Topsports Q2 FY26/27 operating update and interim results (Jun to Aug 2026).** Expected around 2026-10-21 to 22, which is before the Nov 1 submission. A decline narrower than low-teens would be the first partner evidence of a floor. Low-teens or worse confirms SHORT.
- **NBS September 2026 retail sales.** Due around Oct 16 to 20.
- **Pou Sheng October revenue.** Due around Nov 10. It is the cleanest calendar comparison.
- **Anta, Li-Ning and Xtep Q3 updates.** Mid to late October.
- **adidas Q3 2026 Greater China.** About late October.
- **Tmall Double 11 2026 presale and half-time rankings.** From Oct 15 to early November.
- **Nike Investor Day, Nov 16 to 17.** A China revenue bridge for the January 2027 online cut.
- **Not settleable with public data:** Nike-brand sales inside Topsports and Pou Sheng, Nike GMV on Tmall, JD and Douyin, and a Dewu price time series.
