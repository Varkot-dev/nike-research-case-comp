## Recommended pitch changes
1. Lead with the required margin (about 11.6% to 14% EBIT margin to justify the price), then the FY28 bridge, instead of "$0.48 too high".
2. Remove or rewrite: "86%" China and Converse share of declines (D4: gross share is 70%, EMEA 25%); "premium is unjustified"; channel stuffing language; dividend-cut angle.
3. Add an Investor Day silence rule (about 50% chance no numeric FY28 guide) and a pre-written response for each outcome.
4. Check before reuse: "14 of 14 guides". D4 says Q1 FY27 fell below the guide midpoint and missed LSEG consensus. The deck currently says 14 of 14. Verify against the Q1 FY27 release and call before Nov 1.
