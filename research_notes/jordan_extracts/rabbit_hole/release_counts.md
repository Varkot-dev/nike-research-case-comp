# Jordan retro supply test: release counts, 2016 to Oct 2026 (agent report, saved by main session)

Short answer: total Jordan retro releases did not fall in 2024 to Sep 2026. Only AJ1 High shows a clear count cut; AJ4 and AJ3 rose to replace it. The FY2018 reset did not cut release counts either. Release counts cannot separate a deliberate supply cut from weaker sell-through.

Method: 117 Sole Retriever (SR) model archive pages opened via Playwright (AJ1 High/Mid/Low, AJ2 to AJ14), 3,273 entries with name, style code, date, URL. Core adult retro = AJ1 High retro (Zoom CMFT, Element, FlyEase, Method of Make, KO removed), AJ1 Low OG, all AJ2 to AJ14 incl. Lows and women's; excludes kids-only, golf, cleats, slides, AJ1 Mid. Cross-checks: Sneaker Bar Detroit (SBD) half-year archives 2019 to 2026; Sneaker Files 2016 to 2017. SR covers 83 to 95% of SBD retros 2019 to 2026 but only 57 to 72% of Sneaker Files items 2016 to 2018, so pre-2019 counts are minimums.

Jan to Sep only: SR 71, 71, 75, 88, 73 (2022 to 2026); SBD 67, 77, 72, 55, 81.
Nike fiscal years, SR core: FY17 77, FY18 108, FY19 115 (minimums), FY24 98, FY25 113, FY26 95.

Relayed SR figures reproduced: AJ1 High colorways 23 (21 strict) / 15 / 12 vs relayed 20 / 15 / 12; about 8.2 core retros a month Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 (9.2 incl. kids) vs relayed about 10; Feb 2026 "19" is all SR entries, 13 core.
Where volume fell (SR only, 2023 to 2025): AJ1 Mid 52 to 16; AJ1 Low non-OG 78 to 22; AJ1 High Zoom CMFT and similar 40 to 2; kids-only listings 191 to 13 (may reflect SR listing changes).

Implications: (1) 2025 was the highest year since 2018; Hill's Oct 1, 2026 cut is future tense, press expects 2027. (2) AJ1 High count cut real but moderate (about 35 to 50% on SR, less on SBD) while AJ4 tripled and AJ3 roughly doubled: a shift within retro. (3) FY2018 counts rose too; that reset was most likely fewer pairs per release, which archives cannot show. (4) Pairs per release are unobservable and look the same as retailers buying less.

SneakerPing: sneakerping.com/sneaker-resale-market-report (18 Aug 2026) opened; 605 is the Jordan items in its resale-tracked sample (all Jordan lines, Aug 2024 to Aug 2026), not a release census. "More than 700 Jordans in each of 2022 and 2023" appears only in BallerAlert, UNVERIFIED; do not use 700 vs 300 as a supply series.

Holiday 2026 (secondary, VERIFIED): Bred 4, Space Jam 11 (12 Dec), Fireside 12. No reported 2027 lineup or pair counts. Pair counts: AJ11 Concord 2018 989,566 men's plus about 850,000 kids (Complex 21 Mar 2018 leak; number UNVERIFIED); AJ11 Bred 2019 "over a million" (rumour). None found for 2023 to 2026.

Caveats: SR is a tracker, naming inconsistent; do not use SR_AJ1High_OG_adult column. Colorway counts say nothing about pairs. Late 2026 dates may change.
