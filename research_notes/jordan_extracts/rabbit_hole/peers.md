# Peer evidence for the Jordan "supply reset vs demand collapse" thesis

Prepared 2026-10-13. Scope: (1) adidas basketball and lifestyle, franchise supply management, Yeezy; (2) adidas turnaround timeline; (3) basketball market (Li Ning, Anta, Puma, Under Armour/Curry, New Balance); (4) valuation inputs.

Builds on these and does not repeat them: origin/claude/wonderful-franklin-u31n4e `competitor_benchmarks.md` and `peer_multiples.csv`, and origin/claude/stoic-shannon-dmkmyu `competitors_and_turnarounds.md`. Those files had the adidas turnaround at snippet level only (RetailDetail, Paperjam, BusinessDay). This file upgrades the numbers to adidas primary documents. It also adds three things those files lacked: basketball data, EBIT-based multiples, and Amer and Li Ning.

### 1c. How adidas manages franchise supply (company statements = opinion)

**2023, deliberately throttled and then scaled:**
- "While adidas continued to limit the supply at the beginning of the year, the company is slowly starting to scale its offering" (Q1 2023 release p.2).
- Gulden: "We have scaled up supply but are far from covering the total current demand" (Q3 2023 release p.1).
- Both VERIFIED.

**Q4 2025:**
- "focusing on full-price sales and limiting participation in promotional sales events. In addition to protecting the brand and its key lifestyle franchises" (FY 2025 release p.9).
- VERIFIED.

**Q1 2026, Gulden:**
- "heavily discounted in many markets, especially in lifestyle footwear. The discipline of not overselling into retailers is therefore currently crucial" (Q1 2026 release p.1).
- "lifestyle footwear teams are doing a great job extending lifecycles of franchises by updating materials and patterns" (p.2).
- Both VERIFIED.

**Q2 2026:**
- Release: "remained disciplined in its overall sell-in approach, focusing on prudently managing its full-price Lifestyle footwear business in a marketplace characterized by high promotional activity" (release p.3).
- Gulden: "The lifestyle footwear market, especially for him, is currently under pressure due to heavy discounting at many retailers" (p.2).
- Both VERIFIED.
- Call quotes (Investing.com transcript, VERIFIED excerpts):
  - "we were holding back models because we didn't want to go into a discounted environment."
  - On Stan Smith: "we took all these products out of the market so that there shouldn't be any discount."
  - "franchises that used to be full price are now currently at a discount."

**Inventory context:**
- €5,832m at Dec 2025, +17% (+23% currency-neutral) (FY 2025 release p.7).
- €5,969m at June 2026, +13% (Q2 2026 release p.9).
- Both VERIFIED.
- So adidas's lifestyle sell-in restraint is happening while group inventory is still rising.

**Reading:**
- adidas's own explanation of its lifestyle slowdown (from +13% to +2%) is a mix of two things it does not quantify separately: deliberate sell-in restraint, and a discounted market.
- No peer document separates the two.

## 3. Basketball market evidence

**Li Ning, H1 2026 (HKEX interim results, 20 Aug 2026; all VERIFIED):**
- "While the basketball shoe market faced challenges, the Group relied on core signature models combined with an e-commerce-channel-led promotion strategy to keep basketball shoe sales stable" (p.48).
- Way of Wade 12 "overall sell-through rate increased substantially over the previous-generation product" (p.35).
- Group: revenue +2.8% to RMB15,235m; footwear +0.6% (DERIVED); operating margin 16.1% vs 16.5% (p.18).
- Platform retail sell-through up "low-single-digit"; channel inventory-to-sales of 4 months (p.1).

**Li Ning and Curry:**
- Partnership with Stephen Curry and Curry Brand announced on 2 June 2026 (PRNewswire release via AAP, VERIFIED). The release says only "long-term".
- The 10-year term comes from AFP and FashionNetwork (SNIPPET ONLY).

**Under Armour:**
- Curry separation announced on 13 Nov 2025 (8-K Ex. 99.1, VERIFIED): "Curry will become independent of Under Armour"; the Curry 13 is the "final Curry Brand x Under Armour shoe".
- FY2026 10-K (VERIFIED via WebFetch of sec.gov):
  - Footwear $1,076.4m, -10.8%, "primarily due to lower unit sales and lower average selling prices".
  - Total revenue $4,966.4m, -3.8%.
  - Operating loss $163.1m.
  - $69.7m non-cash contract termination costs "primarily relating to the separation of the Curry Brand".
- Turnaround duration: Plank returned in April 2024 and revenue is still falling (consistent with the stoic-shannon file).

**Puma:**
- Basketball was "resilient and delivered healthy growth" in Q3 2025 (Q3 2025 release p.3). Basketball apparel grew in FY 2025 (Annual Report 2025). Both VERIFIED.
- Puma is also mid-reset:
  - Q2 2026 currency-adjusted sales -9.4%, "primarily driven by the reset measures initiated in Q3 2025 and lower consumer demand".
  - Wholesale -14.0%.
  - Inventories -15.3% "mainly due to lower purchasing volumes".
  - Q2 2026 release pp.1-3, VERIFIED.
  - Puma's reset quarters: Q3 25 -10.4%, Q4 25 -20.7%, Q1 26 -1.0%, Q2 26 -9.4%.
- Like adidas, Puma does not split reset from demand.
- Separately, Anta agreed on 27 Jan 2026 to buy a 29.06% stake in Puma (FY 2025 release, VERIFIED).

**Anta:** the 2026 interim mentions only the KAI 3 launch and a Kyrie Irving China tour. No basketball sales figure.

**New Balance:** 2025 sales $9.2bn, +19% (NB release, 19 Feb 2026, VERIFIED). No basketball split.

**US market size:**
- Circana via World Footwear (2 Sep 2026, VERIFIED): H1 2026 US footwear +1% in value; performance +6%; running +13%; "sport lifestyle footwear declined, despite growth in running-inspired styles".
- Circana reported basketball dollar sales +13% for Jan to Aug 2023 (Circana post, VERIFIED).
- No 2024 to 2026 basketball-specific market figure was found. GAP.
