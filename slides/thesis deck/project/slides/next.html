How this thesis was chosen: five candidate theses (two long, three short) were each argued by an advocate and attacked by two skeptics, then ranked by three judges with different lenses (a portfolio manager, an evidence referee and a risk manager). All three picked a short. The final version was then checked claim by claim against Nike's filings, and anything that broke was dropped. Dropped claims include the idea that the Air Jordan 1 supply cut failed and that the stock assumes FY24 margins outright.
