The arithmetic: FY26 revenue was $46,398M. Q1 FY27 was $11,213M, down 4.3%. For the full year to fall 7% to 9%, the remaining three quarters must fall 7.9% to 10.6%. Outside China, the range assumes China falls 26% to 40% for the rest of the year; every scenario is worse than Q1. If Nike meant the guide currency-neutral rather than reported, the numbers improve by about 1 point and the conclusion still holds (outside China becomes -1.0% to -3.1%, still worse than Q1). The $16B Performance figure and its growth are management statements on the call, not audited figures.
