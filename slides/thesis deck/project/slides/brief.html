The call in brief

The stock is priced for margins Nike has not shown it can reach again

1

The price already pays for a recovery

Nike costs about 21 times next year's earnings. Peers cost about 13 times. That gap only makes sense if margins return to their 10-year average or better.

2

The decline is spreading, by Nike's own forecast

To hit its full-year guide, Nike's next three quarters must fall about twice as fast as the last one, and not only in China.

3

The repair keeps slipping

Nike dropped its December 2026 finish date, discounting is back in its filings, and its growth engine, running, sells on sale.

Sources: Nike FY26 10-K, Q1 FY27 10-Q and earnings release; Yahoo Finance peer multiples, Oct 13, 2026

2

The one sentence to remember: Nike's fix is real but narrow, yet the stock is priced as if profit margins will climb back to about their 10-year average or better, while Nike's own forecast shows the sales decline spreading and the repair timetable slipping. We concede the decline was self-inflicted and that North America and running are genuinely improving. Our point is that the price already assumes much more than that.
