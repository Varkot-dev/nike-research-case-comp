How we got the shaded range: a reverse DCF starts from today's share price and asks what long-run profit margin the market must be assuming. WACC is the yearly return investors demand. At a 10% WACC the price needs 13.4% to 14.1%; at 9% it needs 11.4% to 12.2%; at 8% it needs 9.4% to 10.3%. We show 9% and 10% because both are standard professional choices (Kroll and Damodaran equity risk premiums). Plain-English version: if you buy at $35.35 and sales grow 3% a year, you only earn a market-level return (about 9.5% a year) if margins fully return to FY24 levels. Anything less makes Nike a below-market investment.
