Where we differ

The market sees a bottom. We see a narrow fix at a full price.

What the market believes

  • Nike is near the bottom
  • China and Converse are temporary problems
  • Running is back, so the brand is back
  • Margins rebuild once old stock clears
  • Analysts already expect FY28 earnings up about 28%

What we believe

  • The recovery is real in one place: Performance, mainly running and football
  • It is not big enough, or profitable enough, to carry the price
  • Nike's own guide says the next three quarters get worse
  • The clean-up date was quietly dropped

How this differs from the best-known bear. Berenberg (Sell, $27.50) argues Nike's brand has "shifted irreversibly." We do not rely on taste. Our case rests on Nike's own filings and forecast.

Sources: Nike Q1 FY27 10-Q and call; consensus estimates (repo note, Oct 13); Berenberg downgrade, Oct 6, 2026, via press
