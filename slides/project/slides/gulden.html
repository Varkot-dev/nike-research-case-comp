Gulden's playbook, and what Nike lacks

What he did, in order

1. Cleaned up inventory with deliberately low wholesale orders

2. Sold the Yeezy stock instead of writing it off

3. Scaled Samba and Gazelle in steps; rebuilt running around Adizero

4. Cut marketing 8% in 2023, raised it only after margins recovered

5. Guided low and beat: 2023 guide −€700M, actual +€268M

What does not transfer

The Yeezy cushion. About €500M of operating profit over 2023 to 2024; in 2023 it exceeded total reported profit

A lucky trend. The terrace wave was rising as he arrived; it is now fading (footwear +1% in Q2 2026)
