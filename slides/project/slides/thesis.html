Our thesis

Nike is running Gulden's playbook on Plank's clock

The decline is mostly self-inflicted and the fix is working where it was applied first. Expect sustained growth from H2 FY28, not before, and a smaller Nike than the 2024 peak.

Recovery timing is our judgment from the evidence: sustained currency-neutral growth from H2 FY28, around quarters 14 to 15 under Hill; double-digit operating margin around FY29 to FY30; revenue back to FY24's $51.4B around FY31 to FY32. The proof points: peers grew while Nike fell; North America recovered after the reset; Nike wins inside the running trend; the decline sits where Nike's own model was concentrated; and China is a same-store natural experiment against Adidas.
