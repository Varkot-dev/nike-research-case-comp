Hardest objections

What the judges will ask, and our answers

"Two years in, the total is still falling."

The live decline is concentrated: Greater China is about 65% of the Q1 FY27 drop and Converse about 20%. NIKE Brand outside China fell only about 0.8%.

"Nike is just another incumbent losing share."

Partly true over 2016 to 2025 (US share 40.5% to 28.9%). So the target is the incumbent growth line, not the $51B peak.

"Lifestyle sneakers may be in permanent decline."

The biggest open risk. Scarce Nike drops still resell at 27% to 56% premiums, and running-inspired lifestyle grew strong double digits in Q1 FY27.

"Returning CEOs underperform."

The penalty is concentrated in founders and former CEOs. Hill is neither, and fits the Gulden profile.

Sources: Q1 FY27 10-Q and call; Euromonitor via HGI; StockX and resale data, Sept 2026; MIT Sloan Management Review (2020)

Other objections prepared in the report: China may not be fixable on management's timetable (Nike says beyond FY27, external share up to about $0.85B); about 45% of the stock fall is sector de-rating no operating fix reverses; and Nike still trades near 28x guided FY27 EPS, so a recovery is partly priced in.
