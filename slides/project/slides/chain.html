Nike's 10-K calls the 2020 plan "a new digitally empowered phase of the Consumer Direct Offense." Parker stayed Executive Chairman throughout.

Sources: FY21 10-K p. 28; Q4 FY17 call; 2021 proxy p. 35; Nike earnings calls; root cause synthesis

The root date is 2017 under insider CEO Mark Parker, escalated in 2020 under Donahoe. Exogenous shocks masked or amplified it: COVID scarcity, Vietnam closures, the 2021 Xinjiang boycott, the 2022 bullwhip and the resale collapse (Air Jordan 1 resale premium fell from 61% to 4%). The Q1 FY25 cliff of -10.4% preceded Hill's Oct 14, 2024 start.
