The answer

Nike mostly did this to itself, so it is mostly fixable

About 65% to 75% of the headwinds trace to Nike's own decisions. The realistic target is the incumbent growth line, not the FY2024 peak or the 2021 stock price.

Bottom line from the root-cause synthesis. One strategic bet, the Consumer Direct strategy of 2017 to 2023, concentrated the brand in three lifestyle classics sold through a promotional own channel. The revenue fall from FY25 records the unwinding of that bet, magnified in China. External forces are real but secondary.
