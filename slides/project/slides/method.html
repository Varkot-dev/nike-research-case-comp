How we got here

Every explanation was treated as a suspect

01

Investigate

One investigator per cause wrote falsifiable predictions, then tested them against filings and data

02

Challenge

Skeptics tried to disprove each finding, or show it was a symptom of a deeper cause

03

Hunt

A critic searched for unexplained facts and new suspects, which were then investigated too

04

Adjudicate

A lead investigator ruled on every disagreement and built one non-overlapping ledger

23 candidate causes tested. Natural experiments used throughout: which regions, categories, channels and peers did or did not decline, and when.

Full notes: research_notes/Nike decline root cause analysis/ in the team repo

Candidate causes included the DTC pivot, classics oversupply, innovation drought, marketing shift, 2020 reorg and talent loss, China, consumer taste shift, macro and COVID cycle, pricing, tariffs, apparel and women, digital tech, culture, valuation, governance, EMEA and Converse, plus timeline forensics and a peer differential. The critic round added US partner destocking, the Hispanic consumer pullback, the World Cup, the Middle East conflict and tax rate drift.
