Why Nike fell,
and whether it can recover

A root-cause investigation of 23 explanations, benchmarked against Adidas under Gulden and Under Armour under Plank

Nike financials from Nike's own 10-K, 10-Q and earnings filings. All other sources dated and listed in the repo research notes.

Our question: is Nike's decline a self-inflicted execution problem that Elliott Hill's returning insider team can fix, or a structural problem leadership cannot fix? We tested 23 candidate explanations, challenged each with skeptics, and benchmarked Nike against two turnaround precedents.
