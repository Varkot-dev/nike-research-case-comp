General
NIKE Flight B.V. (hereafter "NFBV" or "the Company") was incorporated in the Netherlands in 2018.
The Company is a holding company with the purpose of owning shares of other companies. The principal
activity of the Company is to act as the holding company of other NIKE, Inc. (hereinafter "NIKE, Inc.")
subsidiaries. The Company's subsidiaries are focused on procurement, material management, logistics and
supply chain operations in support of the sale of sports and lifestyle inspired footwear, apparel, equipment
permissible activities. The Company's main subsidiaries and their activities are the ones described below.
NIKE Trading Company B.V. (hereinafter the"NTC") was incorporated in the Netherlands in 2008 and NIKE
Global Trading B.V. (hereinafter the “NGT") was incorporated in the Netherlands in 2007. NTC and NGT
are wholly owned subsidiaries of NIKE Flight B.V. at May 31, 2024. NTC and NGT both have a branch
located in Singapore. The principal activity of NTC and NGT branches is the procurement, material
management, logistics and supply chain operations in support of the sale of sports and lifestyle inspired
footwear, apparel, equipment and accessories. The NTC Branch also provides consultancy services to its

The Company is exposed to market risks,including the effect of changes in foreign currency exchange
rates and interest rates, and uses derivatives to manage financial exposures that occur in the normal course
of business. The Company does not hold or issue derivatives for trading or speculative purposes.
We conduct sale transactions in various currencies, which increases our exposure to the volatility of local
The Company may elect to designate certain derivatives as hedging instruments under the accounting
designated hedging instruments and hedged items, as well as its risk management objectives and
strategies for undertaking hedge transactions. This process includes linking all derivatives designated as
hedges to either recognised assets or liabilities or forecasted transactions.
The Company also utilizes forward exchange contracts and options to manage exchange exposure of
certain non-functional currency denominated monetary assets and liabilities. The purpose of the contracts
and options is to lessenboth the positive and negative effects of currency fluctuations on the Company's
consolidated results of operations, financial position and cash flows.
