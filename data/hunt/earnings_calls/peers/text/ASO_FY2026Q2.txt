Academy Sports and Outdoors, Inc.: ( ASO +2.06% ) reported second-quarter results characterized by top-line momentum and significant margin expansion resulting from tariff refunds. Management stated that the company experienced bifurcated consumer behavior, with high-income household traffic accelerating while low-income shoppers reduced their visit frequency and aggregated purchases around promotional events. The company reinvested a portion of its tariff-related proceeds into lower pricing for private-label products to stimulate demand and drive traffic. Management reported that while comparable sales were slightly negative for the quarter, quarter-to-date trends through Labor Day have improved to low-single-digit positive growth. Academy continues to focus on its store expansion strategy, planning 11 new locations for the third quarter and expanding into high-performance brands such as HOKA to attract affluent consumers.
