This article is a list which contains the top 50 accounts with most followers on the photo and video-sharing social platform [[Instagram]].<ref name="Trackalytics">{{Cite web|url=https://www.trackalytics.com/the-most-followed-instagram-profiles/page/1/|title=The Most Followed Instagram Profiles|website=Trackalytics.com|access-date=April 9, 2020}}</ref><ref name="SocialBlade">{{Cite web|title=Top 50 Most Followed Instagram Business / Creator Accounts (Sorted by Followers Count)|url=https://socialblade.com/instagram/top/50/followers|access-date=January 2, 2021|website=[[Social Blade]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Top 50 Most Followed Instagram accounts in 2021 {{!}} TikTokBlade|url=https://insiflow.com/instagram/top|url-status=live|access-date=2021-06-09|website=Social Media Marketing & Management Dashboard|language=en}}</ref> {{As of|2021|12|lc=y}}, the most-followed individual is Portuguese footballer [[Cristiano Ronaldo]] with over 384 million followers, and the most-followed woman is American reality television star [[Kylie Jenner]] with over 295 million followers. Instagram has its own brand account on the platform, and with over 455&nbsp;million followers, it is the most-followed account overall. ''[[National Geographic]]'' is the second most-followed brand account, with over 199&nbsp;million followers. A total of 32 accounts have exceeded 100&nbsp;million followers on the platform, with 12 of those having exceeded 200&nbsp;million followers.
