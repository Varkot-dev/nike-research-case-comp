During a recent training session at London Colney, the Spanish shot-stopper took to the pitch in an all-white prototype pair. The prototype highlights the heritage-focused direction of the new silo, evoking strong memories of the classic adiPURE 11pro series. Up close, the boot features a premium quilted, cross-stitched leather forefoot engineered for a soft, natural ball touch, paired with traditional central lacing and an adaptive floating tongue. Adidas is retiring the modern Copa line to reinstate adiPURE as its flagship leather touch silo alongside the Predator and F50.
