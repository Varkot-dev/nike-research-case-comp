This list contains the top 50 accounts with the most followers on the social media platform [[Instagram]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://socialblade.com/instagram/top/50/followers|title=Top 50 Instagram users sorted by Followers|access-date=December 4, 2025|website=[[Social Blade]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Most Followed Instagram Accounts in the World in 2025 |url=https://notcommon.com/most-followed/instagram |access-date=March 4, 2025 |website=notcommon.com |language=en |archive-date=March 4, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250304001141/https://notcommon.com/most-followed/instagram |url-status=live}}</ref> {{As of|September 2026}}, Instagram's own account is the most-followed with over 686 million followers; the most-followed person is Portuguese footballer [[Cristiano Ronaldo]] with over 679 million followers.
