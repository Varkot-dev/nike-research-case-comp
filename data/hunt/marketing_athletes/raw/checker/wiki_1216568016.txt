{{Dynamic list}}The top 50 accounts with most followers on the photo- and video-sharing social platform [[Instagram]] are as follows, with each total rounded down to the nearest million followers.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Most Followed Instagram Accounts in the World in 2024 |url=https://notcommon.com/most-followed/instagram |access-date=2024-01-07 |website=notcommon.com |language=en}}</ref> The most-followed account on the platform is Instagram's own brand account. The most followed person is Portuguese footballer [[Cristiano Ronaldo]], and the most followed woman is American singer-actress [[Selena Gomez]].<gallery heights="220" mode="packed">
File:Cristiano Ronaldo playing for Al Nassr FC against Persepolis, September 2023 (cropped).jpg|[[Cristiano Ronaldo]] is the most-followed individual, sportsperson, footballer, Portuguese and European on Instagram, with over 626 million followers.
File:Lionel-Messi-Argentina-2022-FIFA-World-Cup (cropped).jpg|[[Lionel Messi]] is the most-followed Argentinian and South American on Instagram, with over 502 million followers.
File:191125 Selena Gomez at the 2019 American Music Awards.png|[[Selena Gomez]] is the most-followed woman, actress, musician, American and North American on Instagram, with over 429 million followers.
File:Virat Kohli during the India vs Aus 4th Test match at Narendra Modi Stadium on 09 March 2023.jpg|[[Virat Kohli]] is the most-followed Indian, cricketer and Asian individual on Instagram, with over 267 million followers.
File:Official Photograph of Prime Minister Narendra Modi Potrait.png|[[Narendra Modi]] is the most-followed world leader, head of government and politician on Instagram, with over 88.2 million followers.
