Photo by Carla G. Torres for NYRR courtesy of adidas How Many People Finished Each World Marathon Major?  The 2025 New York City Marathon broke the world record for the most finishers in a marathon. It was the second time in the year that the record was broken after the London Marathon beat the previous record.  There were more than 315,000 finishers of the World Marathon Majors in 2025.  MARATHON FINISHERS New York City Marathon 59,226 London Marathon  56,640  Chicago Marathon 54,187 Berlin Marathon 48,020 Tokyo Marathon 36,204 Sydney Marathon 32,959 Boston Marathon 28,409 The Paris Marathon was the third-largest race in 2025 with 55,499 finishers.  How Many Six Star Medallists Were There In 2025? There are now more than 23,000 Six Star finishers and more than 5,500 people claimed their sixth star in 2025, including the marathon running legend Eliud Kipchoge who became the fastest to ever run the races based on his cumulative time.  There are also more than 4,500 people who now have seven stars. You can read more facts about Six Star finishers here including the average marathon finish time, the average age of a six star medallist and the average time it takes for someone to complete all six races. 5 Fastest Men’s Marathons In 2025  Only two men ran under 2:03 in 2025, in a year which came with the promise of a potential new men’s world record – and we came close because in both the Berlin Marathon and the Chicago Marathon, the race leaders went through halfway at 60:16, exactly on the world record split, but faded in the final kilometres. This includes all marathons, not just the Majors. We’re got all the Marathon Major results listed here , plus the World Championship Marathon. Sabastian Sawe ran two of the fastest times in the world in 2025, and was on world record pace at the Berlin Marathon. He was the only person to win two majors this year . In 2026 there will be high expectation on him to go after the marathon world record – and perhaps even challenge the sub-two hour barrier. Jacob Kiplimo finished first and third in his first two career marathons, running the second fastest time in the world this year. He was also on world record pace in the Chicago Marathon. While John Korir won Boston Marathon and after dropping out of the Chicago Marathon returned to win the Valencia Marathon. Conner Mantz broke the American marathon record by running 2:04:43. It’s the fastest marathon ever run by someone who isn’t of African descent.  Hawi Feysa ran 2:14:56 to go into seventh on the all time list. She also came third in the Tokyo Marathon and was one of four women who finished on two World Marathon Major podiums in 2025: Hawi Feysa (🥇🥉), Sifan Hassan (🥇🥉), Hellen Obiri (🥇🥈) and Sharon Lokedi (🥇🥈). The fastest marathon of the year was Joyciline Jepkosgei who ran 2:14:00 at the Valencia Marathon. It’s the fourth-fastest marathon of all time. Just behind her was Peres Jepchirchir in 2:14:43 and sixth on the all-time list. Tigst Assefa set a new women’s only world record in the London Marathon running 2:15:50. Hellen Obiri and Sharon Lokedi once again battled on the hilly courses of Boston and New York City, claiming first and second in both races, with Lokedi winning Boston and Obiri winning New York City. Both wins were also new course records.  Sydney Marathon Joined The World Marathon Majors The six World Marathon Majors became seven as the Sydney Marathon joined the majors.  The Six Star medal will remain and will eventually be joined by a Nine Star medal .  Here’s Mark taking on the Sydney Marathon in its first year as a World Marathon Major.
