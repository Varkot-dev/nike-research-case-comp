                         LATEST OPERATIONAL UPDATE
                        FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023
This announcement is made on a voluntary basis by the board of directors (the “Board”) of Li
Ning Company Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) to
provide the following operational update on the Group’s business.

RETAIL SELL-THROUGH

For the first quarter ended 31 March 2023, the retail sell-through of LI-NING POS (excluding
LI-NING YOUNG) for the overall platform increased by mid-single-digit on a year-on-year
basis. In terms of channels, offline channel (including retail and wholesale) registered a
high-single-digit growth, with retail (direct operation) increased by mid-teens and wholesale
(franchised distributors) increased by mid-single-digit; the e-commerce virtual stores business
registered a low-teens decline.

POS NUMBERS

As at 31 March 2023, the total number of LI-NING POS (excluding LI-NING YOUNG) in
China amounted to 6,206, representing a net decrease of 89 POS since the beginning of this
year. Among the net decrease of 89 POS, direct retail accounts for a net increase of 20 POS,
and wholesale accounts for a net decrease of 109 POS.

As at 31 March 2023, the total number of LI-NING YOUNG POS in China amounted to 1,253,
representing a net decrease of 55 POS since the beginning of this year.

SAME-STORE-SALES (SSS)

For the first quarter ended 31 March 2023, in respect of LI-NING POS (excluding LI-NING
YOUNG) which have been in operation since the beginning of the same quarter last year, the
same-store-sales for the overall platform registered a high-single-digit decline on a
year-on-year basis. In terms of channels, retail channel registered a low-single-digit decline and
wholesale channel registered a low-single-digit decline; while the e-commerce virtual stores
business registered a low-twenties decline on a year-on-year basis.
