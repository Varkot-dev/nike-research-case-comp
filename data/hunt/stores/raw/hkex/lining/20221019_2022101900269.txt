                        LATEST OPERATIONAL UPDATE
                       FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022

This announcement is made on a voluntary basis by the board of directors (the “Board”) of Li
Ning Company Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) to
provide the following operational update on the Group’s business.

SAME-STORE-SALES (SSS)

For the third quarter ended 30 September 2022, in respect of LI-NING POS (excluding
LI-NING YOUNG) which have been in operation since the beginning of the same quarter last
year, the same-store-sales for the overall platform registered a high-single-digit increase on a
year-on-year basis. In terms of channels, retail (direct operation) channel registered a
mid-single-digit increase and wholesale (franchised distributors) channel registered a
high-single-digit increase; while the e-commerce virtual stores business registered a mid-teens
growth on a year-on-year basis.

RETAIL SELL-THROUGH

For the third quarter ended 30 September 2022, the retail sell-through of LI-NING POS
(exclude LI-NING YOUNG) for the overall platform increased by mid-teens on a year-on-year
basis. In terms of channels, offline channel (include retail and wholesale) registered a
mid-teens growth, with retail increased by low-twenties and wholesale increased by low-teens;
the e-commerce virtual stores business registered a mid-twenties growth.

POS NUMBERS

As at 30 September 2022, the total number of LI-NING POS (exclude LI-NING YOUNG) in
China amounted to 6,101, representing a net increase of 164 POS since the end of previous
quarter and a net increase of 166 POS since the beginning of this year. Among the net increase
of 166 POS, direct retail accounts for a net increase of 179 POS, and wholesale accounts for a
net decrease of 13 POS.

As at 30 September 2022, the total number of LI-NING YOUNG POS in China amounted to
1,238, representing a net increase of 63 POS since the end of previous quarter and a net
increase of 36 POS since the beginning of this year.
