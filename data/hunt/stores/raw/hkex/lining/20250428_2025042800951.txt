                         LATEST OPERATIONAL UPDATE
                        FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2025

This announcement is made on a voluntary basis by the board of directors (the “Board”) of Li
Ning Company Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) to provide
the following operational update on the Group’s business.

RETAIL SELL-THROUGH

For the first quarter ended 31 March 2025, the retail sell-through of LI-NING POS (exclude LI-
NING YOUNG) for the overall platform increased by low-single-digit on a year-on-year basis.
In terms of channels, offline channel (include retail and wholesale) registered a low-single-digit
increase, with retail (direct operation) declined by low-single-digit and wholesale (franchised
distributors) increased by a low-single-digit; the e-commerce virtual stores business registered a
low-teens growth.

POS NUMBERS

As at 31 March 2025, the total number of LI-NING POS (exclude LI-NING YOUNG) in China
amounted to 6,088, representing a net decrease of 29 POS since the beginning of this year.
Among the net decrease of 29 POS, direct retail accounts for a net decrease of 6 POS, and
wholesale accounts for a net decrease of 23 POS.

As at 31 March 2025, the total number of LI-NING YOUNG POS in China amounted to 1,453,
representing a net decrease of 15 POS since the beginning of this year.
