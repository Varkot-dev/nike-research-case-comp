                        LATEST OPERATIONAL UPDATE
                      FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2025

This announcement is made on a voluntary basis by the board of directors (the “Board”) of Li
Ning Company Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) to provide
the following operational update on the Group’s business.

RETAIL SELL-THROUGH

For the second quarter ended 30 June 2025, the retail sell-through of LI-NING POS (exclude LI-
NING YOUNG) for the overall platform increased by low-single-digit on a year-on-year basis.
In terms of channels, offline channel (include retail and wholesale) registered a low-single-digit
decline, with retail (direct operation) declined by mid-single-digit and wholesale (franchised
distributors) increased by a low-single-digit; the e-commerce virtual stores business registered a
mid-single-digit growth.

POS NUMBERS

As at 30 June 2025, the total number of LI-NING POS (exclude LI-NING YOUNG) in China
amounted to 6,099, representing a net increase of 11 POS since the end of the previous quarter
and a net decrease of 18 POS since the beginning of this year. Among the net decrease of 18
POS, direct retail accounts for a net decrease of 19 POS, and wholesale accounts for a net
increase of 1 POS.

As at 30 June 2025, the total number of LI-NING YOUNG POS in China amounted to 1,435,
representing a net decrease of 18 POS since the end of the previous quarter and a net decrease
of 33 POS since the beginning of this year.
