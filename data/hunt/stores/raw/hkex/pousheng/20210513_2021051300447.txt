MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Results summary
For the three months ended March 31, 2021, the Group recorded revenue of
approximately RMB7,306.8 million, representing an increase of 47.7% compared with
the same period last year. The net profit attributable to owners of the Company for the
three months ended March 31, 2021 amounted to approximately RMB356.9 million
while there was a net loss attributable to owners of the Company of approximately
RMB165.5 million the same period last year. The turnaround from loss to profit was
mainly due to a quick rebound of the sportswear and sports consumption markets of the
People’s Republic of China after the fading of the novel coronavirus (COVID-19)
pandemic.
