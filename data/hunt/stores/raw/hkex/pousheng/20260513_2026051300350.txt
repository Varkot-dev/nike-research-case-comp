MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Results summary
For the three months ended March 31, 2026 (the “Period”), the Group recorded revenue
of approximately RMB5,051.5 million, representing a decrease of 1.1% compared with
the same period of last year, while the profit attributable to owners of the Company
increased by 32.5% to approximately RMB183.3 million.

During the Period, the Group maintained a solid revenue scale by maintaining a high
degree of agility and flexibility in its decision-making, despite the dynamic retail
landscape in mainland China. This was mainly attributed to the Group’s continuous
enhancement in operational efficiency, dynamic inventory management, and the
establishment of fully integrated, one-stop operations, with resilient performance of
direct retail channel. It continued to strengthen its cross-channel capabilities, strictly
control discounts to improve sales quality, and leverage its restructuring strategy to
enhance synergies, thereby supporting a year-on-year improvement in profitability.

The Group also maintained a strong financial position, underpinned by a robust net cash
balance.
