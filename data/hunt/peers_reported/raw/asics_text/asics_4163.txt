    This is a summary of our financial results for the three months ended March 2026.

    We achieved both revenue and profit growth across all categories and regions.
    Net sales and each stage of profit reached record highs for a quarterly period.

    Consolidated net sales amounted to 270.2 billion yen, representing approximately 30% growth YoY.
    Gross margin was negatively impacted by U.S. tariffs, which were not present in the previous year.
    However, the strong increase in revenue more than offset this impact, resulting in a significant increase
    in operating profit, with the operating margin improving to 22.5% compared with the previous year.

    By category, SportStyle delivered particularly strong performance with approximately 70% growth,
    while Onitsuka Tiger also achieved approximately 34% revenue growth,
    continuing its strong momentum.
    Profitability in both categories further improved YoY.
