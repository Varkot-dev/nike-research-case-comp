                         LATEST OPERATIONAL UPDATE
                        FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2024
This announcement is made on a voluntary basis by the board of directors (the “Board”) of Li
Ning Company Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) to provide
the following operational update on the Group’s business.

RETAIL SELL-THROUGH

For the first quarter ended 31 March 2024, the retail sell-through of LI-NING POS (exclude LI-
NING YOUNG) for the overall platform increased by low-single-digit on a year-on-year basis.
In terms of channels, offline channel (include retail and wholesale) registered a low-single-digit
decline, with retail (direct operation) increased by mid-single-digit and wholesale (franchised
distributors) declined by a mid-single-digit; the e-commerce virtual stores business registered a
low-twenties growth.

POS NUMBERS

As at 31 March 2024, the total number of LI-NING POS (exclude LI-NING YOUNG) in China
amounted to 6,214, representing a net decrease of 26 POS since the beginning of this year.
Among the net decrease of 26 POS, direct retail accounts for a net increase of 1 POS, and
wholesale accounts for a net decrease of 27 POS.

As at 31 March 2024, the total number of LI-NING YOUNG POS in China amounted to 1,405,
representing a net decrease of 23 POS since the beginning of this year.

SAME-STORE-SALES (SSS)

For the first quarter ended 31 March 2024, in respect of LI-NING POS (excluding LI-NING
YOUNG) which have been in operation since the beginning of the same quarter last year, the
same-store-sales for the overall platform registered a mid-single-digit decline on a year-on-year
basis. In terms of channels, retail channel registered a low-single-digit decline and wholesale
channel registered a mid-teens decline; while the e-commerce virtual stores business registered
a low-twenties growth on a year-on-year basis.
