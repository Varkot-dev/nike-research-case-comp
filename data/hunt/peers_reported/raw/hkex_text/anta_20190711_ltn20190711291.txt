       OPERATIONAL UPDATE FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2019

This announcement is published on a voluntary basis by the board of directors (the “Board”)
of ANTA Sports Products Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the
“Group”) to provide an operational update on the business of the Group. The disclosure in
this announcement only relates to the businesses of the Group for ANTA, FILA, DESCENTE,
KOLON SPORT, SPRANDI and KINGKOW brands and does not include businesses of Amer
Sports Corporation.

Retail Sales Performance for the Second Quarter of 2019 for ANTA Brand

The retail sales (in terms of the retail value) of ANTA branded products for the second quarter
of 2019 increased by mid-teens when compared to the same period of 2018.

Retail Sales Performance for the Second Quarter of 2019 for Other Brands

The retail sales (in terms of the retail value) of other branded products for the second quarter
of 2019 increased by 55%-60% when compared to the same period of 2018.

Retail Sales Performance for the First Half of 2019 for ANTA Brand

The retail sales (in terms of the retail value) of ANTA branded products for the first half of
2019 increased by low-teens when compared to the same period of 2018.

Retail Sales Performance for the First Half of 2019 for Other Brands

The retail sales (in terms of the retail value) of other branded products for the first half of
2019 increased by 60%-65% when compared to the same period of 2018.
