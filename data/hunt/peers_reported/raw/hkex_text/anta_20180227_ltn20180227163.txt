 OPERATIONAL UPDATE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2017


This announcement is published on a voluntary basis by the board of directors (the “Board”)
of ANTA Sports Products Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the
“Group”) to provide an operational update on the business of the Group.

Retail Sales Performance for the Fourth Quarter of 2017 for ANTA Brand

The retail sales (in terms of the retail value) of ANTA branded products for the fourth
quarter of 2017 increased by 20%-25% when compared to the same period of 2016.

Retail Sales Performance for the Fourth Quarter of 2017 for Other Brands

The retail sales (in terms of the retail value) of other branded products (excluding brands
newly joined to the Group since 1 October 2016) for the fourth quarter of 2017 increased
by 85%-90% when compared to the same period of 2016.
