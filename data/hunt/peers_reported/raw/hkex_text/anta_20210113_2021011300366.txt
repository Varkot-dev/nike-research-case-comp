        OPERATIONAL UPDATE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER AND
                       FULL YEAR OF 2020
This announcement is published on a voluntary basis by the board of directors (the “Board”) of
ANTA Sports Products Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) to
provide an operational update on the business of the Group. The disclosure in this announcement only
relates to the businesses of the Group for ANTA, FILA, DESCENTE, KOLON SPORT, SPRANDI
and KINGKOW brands and does not include the businesses of Amer Sports Corporation.

Retail Sales Performance for the Fourth Quarter of 2020 for ANTA Brand

Retail sales (in terms of retail value) of ANTA branded products for the fourth quarter of 2020
recorded low single-digit positive growth compared to the same period of 2019.

Retail Sales Performance for the Fourth Quarter of 2020 for FILA Brand

Retail sales (in terms of retail value) of FILA branded products for the fourth quarter of 2020 recorded
25-30% positive growth compared to the same period of 2019.

Retail Sales Performance for the Fourth Quarter of 2020 for other Brands

Retail sales (in terms of retail value) of other branded products for the fourth quarter of 2020 recorded
55-60% positive growth compared to the same period of 2019.

Retail Sales Performance for the Full Year of 2020 for ANTA Brand

Retail sales (in terms of retail value) of ANTA branded products for the full year of 2020 recorded
mid single-digit negative growth compared to the same period of 2019.

Retail Sales Performance for the Full Year of 2020 for FILA Brand

Retail sales (in terms of retail value) of FILA branded products for the full year of 2020 recorded mid
double-digit positive growth compared to the same period of 2019.
