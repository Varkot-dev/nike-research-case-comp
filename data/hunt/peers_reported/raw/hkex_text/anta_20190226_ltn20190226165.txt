    OPERATIONAL UPDATE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER AND
                   FULL YEAR OF 2018


This announcement is published on a voluntary basis by the board of directors (the “Board”)
of ANTA Sports Products Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the
“Group”) to provide an operational update on the business of the Group.

Retail Sales Performance for the Fourth Quarter of 2018 for ANTA Brand

The retail sales (in terms of the retail value) of ANTA branded products for the fourth
quarter of 2018 increased by mid-teens when compared to the same period of 2017.

Retail Sales Performance for the Fourth Quarter of 2018 for Other Brands

The retail sales (in terms of the retail value) of other branded products (including brands
newly joined to the Group after 1 October 2017) for the fourth quarter of 2018 increased by
80%-85% when compared to the same period of 2017.

Retail Sales Performance for the Full Year of 2018 for ANTA Brand

The retail sales (in terms of the retail value) of ANTA branded products for the full year of
2018 increased by mid-teens when compared to the same period of 2017.
